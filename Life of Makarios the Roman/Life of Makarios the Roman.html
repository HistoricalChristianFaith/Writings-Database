Life of Makarios the Roman

No public-domain English translation of this work is available. The first English translation of the Greek Life (BHG 1005), by Stratis Papaioannou, appears in his anthology Saints at the Limits: Seven Byzantine Popular Legends (Dumbarton Oaks Medieval Library 78; Harvard University Press, 2023; ISBN 978-0-674-29079-2), available from the publisher. The Life is translated complete, with Papaioannou’s edition of the Greek text on facing pages; the volume gathers seven popular Byzantine saints’ legends, with an introduction and notes on the text and its manuscripts. Probably composed in the fifth or sixth century, the legend is a fantastic travel tale: three monks of a monastery in Mesopotamia, Theophilos, Sergios and Hyginos, set out to find the place where heaven meets the earth, and journey east through lands of marvels and monsters, among them dog-headed men and pygmies, until near the edge of the world they find the hermit Makarios living in a cave not far from Paradise. He tells them how, the son of a Roman senator, he fled from Rome on his wedding day, was led to the cave by the archangel Raphael, and has lived there ever since. (Noted by Roger Pearse, 21 November 2023.)
