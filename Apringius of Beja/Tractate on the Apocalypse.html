Tractate on the Apocalypse

No public-domain English translation of this work is available. The first English translation of the whole surviving text, by William C. Weinrich, is in Latin Commentaries on Revelation: Victorinus of Petovium, Apringius of Beja, Caesarius of Arles and Bede the Venerable (Ancient Christian Texts; IVP Academic, 2011; ISBN 978-0-8308-2909-5), a volume that also contains the commentaries of Victorinus, Caesarius and Bede; it is available from the publisher. Apringius, bishop of Pax Julia (Beja) in Lusitania, wrote his Tractatus in Apocalypsin in the reign of the Visigothic king Theudis (531–548). It survives incomplete in a single manuscript: only his comments on the opening chapters of Revelation (1:1 to chapter 5) and on the closing chapters (18:6 to the end) are preserved, and Weinrich translates both parts. The commentary explains the text phrase by phrase, chiefly as a description of Christ and his Church. The Latin text was first edited by Marius Férotin (1900) and is now in Roger Gryson’s edition (Corpus Christianorum Series Latina 107, 2003). (Noted by Roger Pearse, 16 June 2009.)
