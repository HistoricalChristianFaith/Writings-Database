Passion of Saint Nicetas

No public-domain English translation of this work is available. The first English translation of this Greek legend of Nicetas (Niketas), son of Maximian, in a redaction related to BHG 1346d, by Stratis Papaioannou, appears in his anthology Saints at the Limits: Seven Byzantine Popular Legends (Dumbarton Oaks Medieval Library 78; Harvard University Press, 2023; ISBN 978-0-674-29079-2), available from the publisher. The Passion is translated complete, with Papaioannou’s edition of the Greek text on facing pages; the volume gathers seven popular Byzantine saints’ legends, with an introduction, which the editor has made available on Academia.edu, and notes on the text and its manuscripts. The legend tells of Nicetas, the son of the emperor Maximian, who becomes a Christian and suffers for his faith; it names the persecuting emperor Dadianus of the legend of St George, and so draws on that story. Some of its scenes are comic: the demon Beelzebub appears, is seized and tormented by the saint, and is made to confess how he leads the faithful astray and stirs up quarrels among them; when he meets Nicetas again later, he takes one look at him and flees. This Nicetas is distinct from the other saints of that name, such as Nicetas the Goth. (Noted by Roger Pearse, 21 November 2023.)
