Histories

No public-domain English translation of this work is available. An annotated English translation of Book III, chapters 1–30, by Brian McLaughlin, An Annotated Translation of John Kantakouzenos’ Histories, Book III, Chapters 1–30 (PhD thesis, Royal Holloway, University of London, 2017), is free to download from the Royal Holloway Research Portal; its licence is not stated. It covers only the first 30 of the 100 chapters of Book III, with an introduction on the manuscripts, the author and his historical method, and a commentary in the form of endnotes. John VI Kantakouzenos (d. 1383), the chief minister of Andronikos III, who after the civil war reigned as emperor beside the young John V (1347–1354), wrote the Histories in four books after his abdication, when he had become the monk Joasaph; it is an account of Byzantine affairs from 1320 to 1356 told largely in his own defence. The chapters translated here open after the death of Andronikos III in June 1341 and cover the outbreak of the civil war of 1341–1347 over the regency for John V. Other English translations: Robert H. Trone, The History of John Kantakouzenos (Book I): Text, Translation and Commentary (PhD dissertation, Catholic University of America, 1979), and Timothy S. Miller, The History of John Cantacuzenus (Book IV): Text, Translation and Commentary (PhD dissertation, Catholic University of America, 1975), both unpublished. (Noted by Roger Pearse, 17 March 2018.)
