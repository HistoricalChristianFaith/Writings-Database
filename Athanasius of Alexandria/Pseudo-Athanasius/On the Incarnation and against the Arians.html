On the Incarnation and against the Arians

No public-domain English translation of this work is available. A complete English translation by Nathan Porter (2024), which he describes as the first in English, is offered free on Academia.edu (archived copy); its licence is not stated. The Greek text is that of Montfaucon’s Benedictine edition, reprinted in Migne, Patrologia Graeca 26, cols. 984–1028. The De incarnatione et contra Arianos (CPG 2806) is a short anti-Arian treatise that has come down among the works of Athanasius but is not by him, and is distinct from his genuine On the Incarnation of the Word. Porter draws attention to its unusual reading of biblical texts and its unfamiliar theological formulas; it has sometimes been credited to Marcellus of Ancyra, probably wrongly. Besides the Greek, it survives in a Latin version in a ninth- or tenth-century manuscript at Florence and in Armenian and Syriac versions. (Noted by Roger Pearse, 9 February 2024.)
