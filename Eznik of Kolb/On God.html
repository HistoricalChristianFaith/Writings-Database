On God

No public-domain English translation of this work is available. The complete work was translated by Monica J. Blanchard and Robin Darling Young as A Treatise on God Written in Armenian by Eznik of Kołb (floruit c. 430–c. 450): An English Translation, with Introduction and Notes (Eastern Christian Texts in Translation 2; Peeters, Leuven, 1998; ISBN 978-90-429-0013-4), from the critical edition of Louis Mariès and Charles Mercier; it is available from the publisher. Eznik of Kołb, one of the first generation of Armenian writers after the invention of the Armenian alphabet and later bishop of Bagrewand, wrote the earliest apologetic treatise in Armenian. It survives without a title in a single manuscript and is known as On God or Refutation of the Sects. In four books it defends the one good Creator against the rival accounts of evil and of the world’s origin given by the pagans and Greek philosophers, the Zurvanite Zoroastrianism of Persia, and Marcion; along the way it preserves unique evidence for fifth-century Zurvanism and Marcionism and for Armenian pagan beliefs. (Noted by Roger Pearse, 2 October 2015.)
