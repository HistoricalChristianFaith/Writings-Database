On Repentance (Latin)

No public-domain English translation of this work is available. An English translation of the Latin sermon by T. S. Pattie is included in his article “Ephraem the Syrian and the Latin manuscripts of De Paenitentia”, British Library Journal 13 (1987), pp. 1–24, which studies the Latin manuscripts of the text. The article can be read free online as a PDF in the British Library’s research repository (doi:10.23636/707); it was formerly on the eBLJ website, now preserved only by the Internet Archive’s Wayback Machine. The repository marks it as in copyright, with the British Library Board as rights holder. The sermon (De paenitentia, also called De patientia; CPL 1143, cf. CPG 3915) belongs to the small collection of sermons that circulated in Latin under the name of “Ephraem the deacon of Edessa”: early Latin translations of texts from the Greek corpus attributed to Ephrem the Syrian (“Ephraem Graecus”), which are probably not by Ephrem himself. The collection survives in manuscripts going back to the seventh and eighth centuries, was widely copied in Western monasteries, and was first printed at Freiburg about 1491. It is a different work from the Syriac Homily on Admonition and Repentance. (Noted by Roger Pearse, 2 November 2018.)
