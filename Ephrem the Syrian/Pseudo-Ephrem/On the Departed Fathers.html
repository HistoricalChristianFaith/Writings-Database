On the Departed Fathers

No public-domain English translation of this work is available. An English translation (1997) by Archimandrite Ephrem Lash, made from the Greek text edited by K. G. Phrantzolas (Thessaloniki, 1989, vol. 2, pp. 9–16), with a short introduction, was published on his website as “On the Departed Fathers” (“Sermon on the Fathers who have Completed their Course”); the site has disappeared, but the page is preserved by the Internet Archive’s Wayback Machine. No licence is given: the page states that its texts and translations are copyright to the translator. The work is one of the Greek sermons transmitted under the name of Ephrem the Syrian (“Ephraem Graecus”), and is the first of two sermons on this subject; it is written in heptasyllabic verse, the “metre of Mar Ephrem”, which Lash renders two half-lines to a line, with paragraph divisions of his own. The speaker grieves that God is gathering his saints out of this world, like a farmer harvesting ripe fruit, while he and his brethren remain idle and unripe; he laments the departed fathers and ascetics, contrasting their virtues with the slackness and quarrelling of his own day, warns that the just are being gathered in so that they may not see the coming tribulation and that the Lord is at the doors, calls his hearers to repent with tears while there is time, and ends by asking them to pray for “the sinner Ephrem, your slack brother”. (Noted by Roger Pearse, 6 November 2018.)
