Sermons

No public-domain English translation of this work is available. The complete collection of 238 sermons edited by Germain Morin was translated by Sister Mary Magdeleine Mueller, O.S.F., as Saint Caesarius of Arles: Sermons in three volumes of The Fathers of the Church: Volume 1 (Sermons 1–80) (FC 31; Fathers of the Church, Inc., 1956; Catholic University of America Press paperback, 2004; ISBN 978-0-8132-1404-7), Volume 2 (Sermons 81–186) (FC 47; Catholic University of America Press, 1964; paperback, 2016; ISBN 978-0-8132-2825-9), and Volume 3 (Sermons 187–238) (FC 66; Catholic University of America Press, 1973; paperback, 2004; ISBN 978-0-8132-1401-6). The copyright of the first volume was renewed in 1984, so it is not in the public domain. Caesarius, bishop of Arles from 502 to 542, preached plainly to the townspeople and country folk of southern Gaul and sent his sermons to other bishops and priests to be read aloud in their own churches. Morin arranges them in five groups: admonitions on Christian life and conduct (1–80), sermons on Scripture, sermons for the seasons and feasts of the Church year, sermons on the saints, and sermons to monks and nuns. Many of them borrow freely from Augustine and other earlier preachers, and they give a vivid picture of everyday religion in the sixth century, including his campaign against surviving pagan customs such as vows at springs and trees, shouting at eclipses of the moon, and refusing to work on Thursday in honour of Jupiter. (Noted by Roger Pearse, 7 January 2014, who quotes Sermon 13, chapter 5, from the first volume.)
