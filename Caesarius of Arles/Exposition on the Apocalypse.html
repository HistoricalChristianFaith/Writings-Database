Exposition on the Apocalypse

No public-domain English translation of this work is available. A complete English translation by William C. Weinrich appears in Latin Commentaries on Revelation (Ancient Christian Texts; IVP Academic, 2011; ISBN 978-0-8308-2909-5), where it is printed with three other Latin commentaries on Revelation, by Victorinus of Petovium, Apringius of Beja and Bede, each with an introduction; it is available from the publisher. The Expositio de Apocalypsi sancti Iohannis is a series of nineteen short homilies working through the Book of Revelation; long transmitted among the works of Augustine, it was restored to Caesarius, bishop of Arles (d. 542), by Germain Morin. Drawing heavily on the lost commentary of the Donatist Tyconius, it reads the visions as a picture of the Church and its struggles in the present age rather than as a timetable of the last days. (Noted by Roger Pearse, 16 June 2009, when the volume was forthcoming.)
