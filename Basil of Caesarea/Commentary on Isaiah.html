Commentary on Isaiah

No public-domain English translation of this work is available. A complete English translation by Nikolai A. Lipatov, St Basil the Great: Commentary on the Prophet Isaiah, has been made available free by the translator on Academia.edu (an archived copy of the page is kept by the Internet Archive's Wayback Machine); its licence is not stated. The translation was first published in print as Commentary on the Prophet Isaiah (Texts and Studies in the History of Theology 7; Mandelbachtal and Cambridge: Edition Cicero, 2001; ISBN 978-3-934285-39-2), the first English translation of the work. The commentary (Enarratio in prophetam Isaiam, CPG 2911; Migne, Patrologia Graeca 30) works verse by verse through the first sixteen chapters of Isaiah. It has come down under Basil's name, but its authenticity has been doubted since the Benedictine edition, which printed it among his doubtful works; Lipatov defends Basil's authorship in a companion study. (Noted by Roger Pearse, 5 December 2017.)
