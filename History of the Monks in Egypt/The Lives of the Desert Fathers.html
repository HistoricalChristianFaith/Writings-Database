The Lives of the Desert Fathers

No public-domain English translation of this work is available. The first English translation of the Greek text, by Norman Russell, with an introduction by Benedicta Ward, is The Lives of the Desert Fathers: The Historia Monachorum in Aegypto (Cistercian Studies 34; Cistercian Publications, Kalamazoo, and Mowbray, London, 1981; ISBN 978-0-87907-934-5); it is a complete translation and is available from the publisher. The Historia monachorum in Aegypto is the anonymous account of a journey made in 394–395 by seven monks from Palestine, who travelled up the Nile to visit the famous ascetics of the Egyptian desert. It describes in turn the monks they met or heard of, among them John of Lycopolis, Apollo, Or and Ammon, with their sayings, way of life and miracles. Rufinus of Aquileia made a free Latin version of it, which was later counted among the Lives of the Fathers, and a Syriac version, ascribed to Jerome, is included in E. A. Wallis Budge’s public-domain translation of ʿAnan-Ishoʿ’s Book of Paradise (1904), listed in this collection under the Sayings of the Desert Fathers. (Noted by Roger Pearse, 4 May 2012.)
