Homily 16 on Baptism at the Easter Vigil

No public-domain English translation of this work is available. The first English translation of this homily, by Nathan Porter (2024), made from Marcel Richard’s edition of the Greek text (1956), is offered free as a PDF on Academia.edu. Its licence is not stated. The homily is no. 16 of a collection of thirty-one Greek homilies on the Psalms (CPG 2815), several of them preached on the eight days of Easter, which Richard attributed to Asterius “the Sophist” of Cappadocia, the early fourth-century ally of Arius and of Eusebius of Caesarea; if that attribution is right, it may date from the 330s, although Wolfram Kinzig has argued that the collection is the work of a later, Nicene preacher of the same name, writing around 400. It is addressed to those newly baptized at the Easter Vigil. Other English translations: Nathan Porter has also translated Homily 14, the first homily on Psalm 8, “On the Newly Baptized as Cicadas”, likewise free on Academia.edu; short selections from the homilies appear in Craig A. Blaising and Carmen S. Hardin (eds.), Psalms 1–50 (Ancient Christian Commentary on Scripture, Old Testament 7; IVP Academic, 2008; ISBN 978-0-8308-1477-0), available from the publisher; and Roger Pearse has posted English renderings of the opening of the first homily on Psalm 8 (from Joseph Paramelle’s French) and of the passage on Matthew 27:25 in Homily 21, sections 13–14 (translated by “Inepti graeculi”). Kinzig has translated the whole collection into German (Asterius: Psalmenhomilien, 2002). (Noted by Roger Pearse, 27 March 2024.)
