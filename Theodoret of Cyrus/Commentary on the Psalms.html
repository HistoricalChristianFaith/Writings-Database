Commentary on the Psalms

No public-domain English translation of this work is available. The complete work was translated by Robert C. Hill as Theodoret of Cyrus: Commentary on the Psalms in two volumes (The Fathers of the Church 101–102; Catholic University of America Press): Psalms 1–72 (FC 101, 2000; ISBN 978-0-8132-0101-6) and Psalms 73–150 (FC 102, 2001; ISBN 978-0-8132-0102-3), with an introduction and notes by the translator. Theodoret, bishop of Cyrus in Syria and one of the last great exegetes of the Antiochene tradition, wrote this commentary on the whole Psalter in the middle of the fifth century. He works through each psalm in turn, reading it first in its historical setting in the life of David and of Israel, but, unlike some of his Antiochene predecessors, he also finds in many psalms prophecies of Christ and the Church, steering between a purely historical reading and unrestrained allegory. The Greek text is printed in the Patrologia Graeca, vol. 80. (Noted by Roger Pearse, 8 June 2015; see also his posts of 25 May 2015 and 10 July 2015.)
