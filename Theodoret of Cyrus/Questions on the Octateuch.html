Questions on the Octateuch

No public-domain English translation of this work is available. The complete work was translated by Robert C. Hill, with the Greek text revised by John F. Petruccione, as Theodoret of Cyrus: The Questions on the Octateuch in two volumes (The Library of Early Christianity 1–2; Catholic University of America Press, 2007): vol. 1, On Genesis and Exodus (ISBN 978-0-8132-1498-6; paperback 978-0-8132-1499-3) and vol. 2, On Leviticus, Numbers, Deuteronomy, Joshua, Judges, and Ruth (ISBN 978-0-8132-1500-6; paperback 978-0-8132-1501-3), with an introduction and notes. A single excerpt, Question 1 on Deuteronomy, also appears in English as a lectionary reading in A Word in Season, vol. II (2nd ed., Augustinian Press, 1999). Written in the last years of Theodoret's life, the work covers the eight historical books from Genesis to Ruth in the form of questions and answers: rather than commenting on every verse, it takes up the passages most likely to puzzle or mislead readers, such as apparent contradictions, difficult laws and questions about God's nature, and answers them from the biblical text, from Theodoret's knowledge of Jewish practice and from earlier interpreters. (Noted by Roger Pearse, 10 July 2015; see also his post of 26 March 2018.)
