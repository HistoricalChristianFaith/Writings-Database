Commentary on the Twelve Prophets

No public-domain English translation of this work is available. The complete work was translated by Robert C. Hill as Theodoret of Cyrus: Commentary on the Twelve Prophets (Commentaries on the Prophets, vol. 3; Holy Cross Orthodox Press, Brookline, Mass., 2006; ISBN 978-1-885652-76-8), with an introduction and notes by the translator; it is available from the publisher. The commentary covers the twelve minor prophets, from Hosea to Malachi, verse by verse. In the manner of the school of Antioch Theodoret explains each prophecy first in the history of Israel and Judah, from the Assyrian and Babylonian conquests to the return from exile, while also finding in the prophets passages fulfilled in Christ and the Church. The translator's introduction discusses Theodoret's biblical text and his attitude to the Jews. The Greek text is printed in the Patrologia Graeca, vol. 81. (Noted by Roger Pearse, 10 July 2015.)
