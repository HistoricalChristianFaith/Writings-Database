Religious History

No public-domain English translation of this work is available. The complete work was translated by R. M. Price as A History of the Monks of Syria (Cistercian Studies 88; Cistercian Publications, Kalamazoo, 1985; ISBN 978-0-87907-988-8), with an introduction and notes; it is available from the publisher (Cistercian Publications is now an imprint of Liturgical Press). The Religious History (Philotheos historia), written by Theodoret around 440, is a collection of thirty lives of the hermits and ascetics of Syria from the fourth and early fifth centuries, many of whom Theodoret knew personally or heard about from their disciples; it includes the earliest account of Symeon Stylites, written while the saint was still alive on his pillar, and ends with a discourse on divine love. It is one of the chief sources for early Syrian monasticism. (Noted by Roger Pearse, 20 November 2013; see also his posts of 23 November 2013 and 23 November 2013.)
