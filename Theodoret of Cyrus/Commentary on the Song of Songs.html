Commentary on the Song of Songs

No public-domain English translation of this work is available. The complete work was translated by Robert C. Hill as Theodoret of Cyrus: Commentary on the Song of Songs (Early Christian Studies 2; Centre for Early Christian Studies, Australian Catholic University, Brisbane, 2001; ISBN 0-9577483-2-9), with an introduction and commentary by the translator; it is listed on the publisher's page (archived copy), which directs orders to Sydney College of Divinity Press. The commentary is among Theodoret's earliest works of biblical interpretation. Against readers who took the Song as a merely human love poem and doubted its place in Scripture, he defends it as an inspired book and reads it as a spiritual allegory of the love between Christ and his bride, the Church, drawing in this on the allegorical tradition of Origen rather than on the literal approach usual in his own school of Antioch. The Greek text is printed in the Patrologia Graeca, vol. 81. (Noted by Roger Pearse, 10 July 2015.)
