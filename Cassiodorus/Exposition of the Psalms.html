Exposition of the Psalms

No public-domain English translation of this work is available. The complete work was translated by P. G. Walsh as Cassiodorus: Explanation of the Psalms in three volumes (Ancient Christian Writers; Paulist Press, New York/Mahwah, NJ): Volume 1, Psalms 1–50 (ACW 51, 1990; ISBN 978-0-8091-0441-3), Volume 2, Psalms 51–100 (ACW 52, 1991; ISBN 978-0-8091-0444-4), and Volume 3, Psalms 101–150 (ACW 53, 1991; ISBN 978-0-8091-0445-1), with the translator’s introduction and notes. Like the printed Latin text it is made from, the translation leaves out the marginal symbols (notae) with which Cassiodorus marked passages touching on doctrine, definitions, figures of speech, etymologies, rhetoric, logic and the liberal arts. The Expositio Psalmorum (CPL 900) is a long commentary on all one hundred and fifty psalms, drawing heavily on Augustine’s Enarrationes in Psalmos: for each psalm Cassiodorus explains the title, divides the psalm into its parts, expounds it verse by verse, and closes with a short conclusion, pointing out along the way how Scripture already uses the arts of secular learning. It was originally arranged in three volumes of fifty psalms each. (Noted by Roger Pearse, 12 September 2019.)
