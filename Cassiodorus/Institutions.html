Institutions

No public-domain English translation of this work is available. The complete work was translated by James W. Halporn, with an introduction by Mark Vessey, as Cassiodorus: Institutions of Divine and Secular Learning and On the Soul (Translated Texts for Historians 42; Liverpool University Press, 2004; ISBN 978-0-85323-998-7); the volume also contains Cassiodorus’s short treatise On the Soul, and is available from the publisher. An earlier translation by Leslie Webber Jones, An Introduction to Divine and Human Readings (Columbia University Press, 1946), is not freely available online. The Institutiones divinarum et saecularium litterarum were written by Cassiodorus for the monks of his monastery at Vivarium in southern Italy. The first book is a guide to the study of Scripture: it goes through the books of the Bible, naming the commentators and other Christian writers to be read on each, and gives advice on the careful copying and correcting of manuscripts and on the monastic life. The second book is a short handbook of the seven liberal arts (grammar, rhetoric, dialectic, arithmetic, music, geometry and astronomy), meant as an aid to understanding Scripture. (Noted by Roger Pearse, 16 September 2024.)
