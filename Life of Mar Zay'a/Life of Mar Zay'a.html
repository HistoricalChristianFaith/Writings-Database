Life of Mar Zay‘a

No public-domain English translation of this work is available. The first English translation, by David Wilmshurst, is Mar Zayʿa: The Saint of Jilu (Texts from Christian Late Antiquity 85; Piscataway, NJ: Gorgias Press, 2026; ISBN 978-1-4632-0620-8), which gives the Syriac text with the translation and a substantial commentary on the evidence for early Christian settlement in the Hakkari region; it is available from the publisher. The translation covers the complete Life. Mar Zay‘a is a legendary holy man from Palestine, said to have lived in the fourth century, whom the Church of the East credits with converting the pagan villages of the Sapna valley and of Jilu in the Hakkari mountains (now in south-eastern Turkey). The Syriac Life, by an unknown author from Jilu, probably a bishop or priest writing in the eighteenth century, was written to honour the saint’s church in the village of Matha d’Mar Zay‘a; it tells the story of his journeys and miracles and is rich in local topography, and it was edited by Paul Bedjan in Acta Martyrum et Sanctorum, vol. 1 (Paris, 1890). (Noted by Roger Pearse, 13 October 2026.)
