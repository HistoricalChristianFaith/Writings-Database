Encomium on the Holy Martyrs

No public-domain English translation of this work is available. A free draft English translation (version 0.1) by the scholar who writes as “Inepti Graeculi”, made from the Greek text edited by Michel Aubineau (1975), is posted on Academia.edu as “Ps.-Chrysostom, Encomium in Sanctos Martyres (CPG 4759, BHG 1191e): Draft Translation” (a free Academia.edu login is needed to download it). Its licence is not stated. The work (CPG 4759; BHG 1191e) is a Greek panegyric in praise of the martyrs, one of the many sermons transmitted under the name of John Chrysostom but not by him. (Noted by Roger Pearse, 24 January 2015.)
