Prayer for the Possessed

No public-domain English translation of this work is available. A free draft English translation (version 0.2, 2015) by the scholar who writes as “Inepti Graeculi”, made from the Greek text in the Patrologia Graeca (vol. 64, col. 1061), is posted on Academia.edu as “Ps.-Chrysostom, Precatio in Obsessos (PG 64.1061 = CPG 4710): Draft Translation” (a free Academia.edu login is needed to download it). Its licence is not stated. The work (CPG 4710; in Migne Precatio in obsessos) is a short Greek prayer for those possessed by demons, ascribed to John Chrysostom but not by him. Montfaucon took it from Goar’s Euchologion sive Rituale Graecorum (Paris, 1647, p. 783) and printed it, with several other prayers, as a supplement to the Liturgy that bears Chrysostom’s name, from which Migne reprinted it; it is not in the earlier Chrysostom editions of Savile and Fronton du Duc, and it is still found in the service books of the Eastern Orthodox Church. (Noted by Roger Pearse, 24 January 2015.)
