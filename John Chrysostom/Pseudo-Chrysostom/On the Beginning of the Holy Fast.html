On the Beginning of the Holy Fast

No public-domain English translation of this work is available. A free draft English translation (version 0.1) by the scholar who writes as “Inepti Graeculi”, made from the Greek text in the Patrologia Graeca (vol. 62, cols. 727–728), is posted on Academia.edu as “Ps. Chrysostom, In Ingressum sanctorum jejuniorum 1 (PG 62:727–728 = CPG 4665): Draft Translation” (a free Academia.edu login is needed to download it). Its licence is not stated. The work (CPG 4665; in Migne the first of the homilies In ingressum sanctorum jejuniorum, “On the beginning of the holy fasts”) is a short Greek homily for the opening of Lent, on fasting and against drunkenness, transmitted under the name of John Chrysostom but not by him; it has been ascribed to Proclus of Constantinople (by Marx, Leroy and de Aldama) or to an anonymous rhetorician of the sophistic school (by Musurillo). (Noted by Roger Pearse, 24 January 2015.)
