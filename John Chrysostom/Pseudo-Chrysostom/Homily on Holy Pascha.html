Homily on Holy Pascha

No public-domain English translation of this work is available. A free English version (2025) by Roger Pearse, made not from the Greek but from Nathalie Rambault’s French translation in her critical edition (Jean Chrysostome, Homélies sur la Résurrection, l’Ascension et la Pentecôte, t. 1, Sources Chrétiennes 561, Paris: Cerf, 2013, pp. 267–301), is posted on his blog as a PDF and a Word document. Its licence is not stated. The work (CPG 4408, Homilia in sanctum pascha; PG 52, cols. 765–772) is one of a number of Easter homilies transmitted under the name of John Chrysostom; according to its latest editor it is not genuine, but was put together between the end of the sixth and the middle of the eighth century from extracts of Chrysostom’s works, for use in the Easter services. It survives in a long recension, a revised form preserved in a single manuscript, and a short recension, and was also translated into Old Slavonic. Other English translations: J. Fotopoulos, “John Chrysostom: On Holy Pascha”, Greek Orthodox Theological Review 37 (1992), pp. 123–134, a complete translation. (Noted by Roger Pearse, 31 October 2025.)
