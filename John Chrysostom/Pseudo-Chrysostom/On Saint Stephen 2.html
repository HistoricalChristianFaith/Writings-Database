On Saint Stephen 2

No public-domain English translation of this work is available. A free draft English translation (version 0.1, 2015) by the scholar who writes as “Inepti Graeculi”, made from the Greek text in the Patrologia Graeca (vol. 63, cols. 931–934), is posted on Academia.edu as “Ps.-Chrysostom, In Sanctum Stephanum 2 (PG 63:931–934 = CPG 4691): Draft Translation” (a free Academia.edu login is needed to download it). Its licence is not stated. The work (In sanctum Stephanum 2, CPG 4691) is a short Greek homily on Stephen, the first martyr, one of several sermons on him that have come down under the name of John Chrysostom but are not by him, and which Migne prints among Chrysostom’s spurious works. (Noted by Roger Pearse, 24 January 2015.)
