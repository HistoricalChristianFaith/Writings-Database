On the River Jordan

No public-domain English translation of this work is available. A free draft English translation (version 0.1, 2015) by the scholar who writes as “Inepti Graeculi”, made from the Greek text in the Patrologia Graeca (vol. 61, cols. 725–728), is posted on Academia.edu as “Ps.-Chrysostom, In Jordanem fluvium (PG 61.725–28 = CPG 4648): Draft Translation” (a free Academia.edu login is needed to download it). Its licence is not stated. The work (CPG 4648; in Migne In Jordanem fluvium) is a short Greek homily on the river Jordan, transmitted among the works of John Chrysostom but not by him; B. Marx (1939) assigned it to Severian of Gabala, but H.-D. Altendorf (1957) rejected the attribution, and its author remains unknown. (Noted by Roger Pearse, 24 January 2015.)
