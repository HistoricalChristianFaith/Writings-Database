Homilies on Genesis

No public-domain English translation of this work is available. The complete series of sixty-seven homilies was translated by Robert C. Hill in three volumes of The Fathers of the Church (Catholic University of America Press): Homilies on Genesis 1–17 (FC 74, 1986; paperback ISBN 978-0-8132-0972-2), Homilies on Genesis 18–45 (FC 82, 1990; paperback ISBN 978-0-8132-1087-2), and Homilies on Genesis 46–67 (FC 87, 1992; paperback ISBN 978-0-8132-1496-2), with an introduction and notes; it is the first English translation. Chrysostom preached the series at Antioch in the 380s, while still a priest, partly during Lent and partly after Pentecost, working through the book verse by verse from the creation to the story of Joseph and drawing moral lessons for his congregation from the lives of the patriarchs Abraham, Isaac, Jacob and Joseph. It is his longest series on any book of the Old Testament, and is distinct from his shorter set of Sermons on Genesis. (Noted by Roger Pearse, 10 July 2015.)
