Praise of Diodore

No public-domain English translation of this work is available. A free draft English translation (2015) by Pearse’s correspondent “IG”, the scholar who writes as “Inepti Graeculi”, made from the Greek text in the Patrologia Graeca (vol. 52, cols. 761–766), is posted on Academia.edu as “Chrysostom, Laus Diodori (PG 52.761–766 = CPG 4406): Draft Translation”, without commentary (a free Academia.edu login is needed to download it). Its licence is not stated. The work (CPG 4406; in Migne Laus Diodori episcopi) is a short sermon preached by Chrysostom at Antioch, while he was still a priest there, in honour of Diodore, bishop of Tarsus, who had been his teacher; among other things he recalls how Diodore fed the orthodox congregation of Antioch with sound doctrine under the emperor Valens, when they were driven out of their churches, likening his preaching to a lyre and to the trumpets of Jericho. (Noted by Roger Pearse, 4 February 2015.)
