On His Return from Asia

No public-domain English translation of this work is available. The only English translation, by Wendy Mayer and Pauline Allen, is “On His Return”, in their John Chrysostom (The Early Church Fathers; London: Routledge, 2000; ISBN 978-0-415-18252-2 hardback, 978-0-415-18253-9 paperback), pp. 98–103, an anthology of Chrysostom’s sermons and letters with an introduction to his life and preaching; it is a complete translation of the sermon, and the book is available from the publisher. The sermon (De regressu, CPG 4394; Latin version only in PG 52, cols. 421–424; Greek text first published by Wenger in Revue des Études byzantines 19, 1961, pp. 110–123, as the homily “à son retour d’Asie”) was preached at Constantinople, probably in the spring of 402, when Chrysostom came back from his journey to Asia Minor, where he had deposed bishops accused of simony at Ephesus; it opens with Moses, “the great servant of God”. It is distinct from the sermons he preached on returning from his first exile in 403. (Noted by Roger Pearse, 28 April 2010; see also his post of 17 August 2012.)
