Demonstration against the Pagans that Christ is God

No public-domain English translation of this work is available. The complete work was translated by Paul W. Harkins as “Demonstration against the Pagans that Christ is God”, in Apologist (The Fathers of the Church 73; Catholic University of America Press, 1985; paperback ISBN 978-0-8132-1086-5), pp. 153 ff., where it follows Margaret A. Schatkin’s translation of the Discourse on Blessed Babylas; Harkins based his version on the unpublished critical Greek text of Norman G. McKendrick, and the book is available from the publisher. McKendrick’s own English translation, with his critical text, is in his unpublished Fordham University PhD dissertation, The “Quod Christus Sit Deus” of John Chrysostom (1966). The work (in Greek and Latin titled Against Jews and Pagans, that Christ is God, Contra Iudaeos et gentiles quod Christus sit Deus; Migne, Patrologia Graeca 48:813 ff.) is one of Chrysostom’s early writings: he argues that Christ is God from the fulfilment in him of the prophecies of the Old Testament, and from Christ’s own prophecies, above all his prediction that the Church would spread through the whole world and could not be overcome, a growth that he holds only divine power could bring about. (Noted by Roger Pearse, 29 November 2011; see also his post of 21 October 2017, which quotes chapter 5 from Harkins’s version.)
