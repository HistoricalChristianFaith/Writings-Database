Commentary on the Psalms

No public-domain English translation of this work is available. The complete surviving commentary was translated by Robert Charles Hill as St. John Chrysostom: Commentary on the Psalms, in two volumes, with an introduction and notes (Brookline, MA: Holy Cross Orthodox Press, 1998): Volume 1 (ISBN 978-1-885652-12-6) and Volume 2 (ISBN 978-1-885652-25-6), available from the publisher’s bookstore. Chrysostom’s expositions of the Psalms, probably given at Antioch, survive for only fifty-eight psalms, though he may have covered the whole Psalter; they explain the text verse by verse in the literal and historical manner of the Antiochene school, often cite the readings of the other Greek versions, and draw out moral lessons for his hearers. Hill, an Australian scholar who after his retirement translated many of the Antiochene Fathers, also translated Chrysostom’s homilies on Genesis, his Old Testament homilies and his commentaries on the Wisdom books. (Noted by Roger Pearse, 10 July 2015.)
