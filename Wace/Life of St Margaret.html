Life of St Margaret

No public-domain English translation of this work is available. The complete poem was translated by Jean Blacker, Glyn S. Burgess and Amy V. Ogden in Wace, The Hagiographical Works: The Conception Nostre Dame and the Lives of St Margaret and St Nicholas (Studies in Medieval and Reformation Traditions 169 / Texts and Sources 3; Brill, Leiden, 2013; ISBN 978-90-04-24705-5), where it occupies pp. 147–234, with an introduction and notes and printed with the Old French text; it is available from the publisher. The volume brings together all three of Wace's saints' poems and gives the first English translations of them. La Vie de sainte Marguerite is a twelfth-century Old French verse life of Margaret of Antioch, the virgin martyr, by the Norman poet Wace, adapted from a Latin passion of the saint. It tells of her refusal to marry the pagan prefect Olibrius, her imprisonment, her struggle with the devil in the form of a dragon, her tortures and her death by beheading. (Noted by Roger Pearse, 6 December 2019.)
