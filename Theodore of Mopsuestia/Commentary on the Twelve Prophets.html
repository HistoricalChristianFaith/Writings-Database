Commentary on the Twelve Prophets

No public-domain English translation of this work is available. The complete commentary was translated by Robert C. Hill as Theodore of Mopsuestia: Commentary on the Twelve Prophets (The Fathers of the Church 108; Catholic University of America Press, 2004; ISBN 978-0-8132-0108-5; paperback reprint 2014, ISBN 978-0-8132-2643-9), the first English translation of the work, with an introduction on Theodore's life, his biblical text, and his approach to Scripture; it is available from the publisher. The commentary works through the Minor Prophets from Hosea to Malachi in the plain historical manner of the school of Antioch, reading most of the prophecies against the history of Israel rather than as direct predictions of Christ. It is the only one of Theodore's many biblical commentaries to survive complete in the original Greek, his writings having largely perished after his condemnation at the Council of Constantinople in 553. (Noted by Roger Pearse, 10 July 2015.)
