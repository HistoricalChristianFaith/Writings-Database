Sentences

No public-domain English translation of this work is available. The complete work was translated by Giulio Silano as The Sentences in four volumes (Mediaeval Sources in Translation; Pontifical Institute of Mediaeval Studies, Toronto): Book 1: The Mystery of the Trinity (MST 42, 2007; ISBN 978-0-88844-292-5), Book 2: On Creation (MST 43, 2008; ISBN 978-0-88844-293-2), Book 3: On the Incarnation of the Word (MST 45, 2008; ISBN 978-0-88844-295-6), and Book 4: On the Doctrine of Signs (MST 48, 2010; ISBN 978-0-88844-296-3), each with an introduction by the translator; it is the first complete English translation. Compiled at Paris in the 1150s, the Sentences gathers the opinions of the Fathers, above all Augustine, on the main questions of Christian doctrine and arranges them in four books: God and the Trinity; creation, angels, man and sin; the Incarnation, redemption and the virtues; and the sacraments and the last things. It became the standard textbook of theology in the medieval universities, on which every master was expected to lecture and write a commentary. (Noted by Roger Pearse, 17 August 2023.)
