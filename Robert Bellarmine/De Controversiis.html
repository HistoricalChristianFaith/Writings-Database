De Controversiis

No public-domain English translation of this work is available. A translation of the entire work by Ryan Grant is being published in separate volumes by Mediatrix Press, beginning with On the Roman Pontiff (2015–2016; one-volume revised edition ISBN 978-0-692-70570-4); the volumes issued so far also include On Councils, On the Church Militant, On the Marks of the Church and On Purgatory, while the treatises on the Church Triumphant, clergy, monks and laity, the sacraments, grace, free will and justification, and indulgences are still in progress or planned. The publisher’s Bellarmine Translation Project page lists the volumes published and the order of work. It is therefore a partial translation so far. Kenneth Baker, S.J., translated the first three controversies (on the Word of God, on Christ, and on the Sovereign Pontiff) as Controversies of the Christian Faith (Keep the Faith, 2016; ISBN 978-0-9912268-6-3), reissued in separate volumes in 2025. The Disputationes de Controversiis Christianae Fidei adversus huius temporis haereticos, first published at Ingolstadt in 1586–1593, grew out of the lectures Bellarmine gave at the Roman College; it sets out the Catholic position, with the arguments from Scripture and the Fathers, on each of the main points disputed with the Protestant reformers: the Word of God and its interpretation, Christ, the pope, councils, the members of the Church, purgatory, the veneration of the saints, the sacraments, grace and free will, justification and good works. The Latin text is in the Paris edition of his Opera omnia (Vivès, 1870–1874), vols. 1–6. (Noted by Roger Pearse, 3 March 2022.)
