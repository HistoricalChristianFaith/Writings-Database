Poems

No public-domain English translation of this work is available. The complete poems were translated by P. G. Walsh as The Poems of St. Paulinus of Nola, with an introduction and notes (Ancient Christian Writers 40; Newman Press, 1975; ISBN 978-0-8091-0197-9). The book is no longer listed by its publisher, Paulist Press; a copy can be borrowed from the Internet Archive. Walsh translates the whole collection of Paulinus’ Latin verse as numbered in Hartel’s edition (CSEL 30), the last of them written by about 409: the early verse letters, including the exchange with his old teacher Ausonius in which Paulinus defends his renunciation of the world; paraphrases of Psalms; the series of birthday poems (natalicia) composed each year for the feast of St. Felix on 14 January, which tell the saint’s life and miracles and describe the pilgrims and buildings at his shrine in Nola; a farewell poem for Nicetas of Remesiana; a Christian wedding poem; and a consolation on the death of a young boy. The collection also includes some poems of doubtful authorship, among them the anonymous anti-pagan Poem to Antonius (carm. 32). (Noted by Roger Pearse, 11 December 2009.)
