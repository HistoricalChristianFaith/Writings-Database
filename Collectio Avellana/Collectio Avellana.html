Collectio Avellana

No public-domain English translation of this work is available. A selection of its documents is translated in P. R. Coleman-Norton, Roman State and Christian Church: A Collection of Legal Documents to A.D. 535 (3 vols; SPCK, London, 1966; reissued by Wipf and Stock, 2018; ISBN 978-1-5326-4451-1 for the three-volume set), an anthology of imperial and other legal documents bearing on the Church, each with notes; the reissue is available from the publisher. Among the fourth- and early fifth-century documents it includes many of nos. 2a–40 of the collection — the imperial letters on the schism between Damasus and Ursinus, on the Luciferians and on the building of St Paul’s basilica, and most of the documents on the disputed papal election of Boniface and Eulalius in 418–419 — as tabulated on the Fourth Century Christianity website, which also offers its own translation of document 1. The Collectio Avellana, named after the monastery of Santa Croce di Fonte Avellana where one of its manuscripts was kept, is a collection of 244 documents dated between 367 and 553, mostly letters of emperors and popes and imperial acts, gathered soon after the middle of the sixth century; many of them survive nowhere else. (Noted by Roger Pearse, 28 March 2011.)
