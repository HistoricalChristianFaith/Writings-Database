On the Thebaid

No public-domain English translation of this work is available. The first English translation, by Leslie George Whitbread, is in Fulgentius the Mythographer (Ohio State University Press, 1971; ISBN 0-8142-0162-8), pp. 233–243, a volume that translates, with introductions and notes, all five Latin works ascribed to Fulgentius the mythographer, following Rudolf Helm’s Teubner edition (1898). The publisher has made the book freely available through the Ohio State University Knowledge Bank (book page, PDF); the printed book is marked “All rights reserved”, and no open licence is stated for the free copy. Fabius Planciades Fulgentius was a Latin writer of Vandal North Africa in the late fifth or early sixth century, sometimes, though not certainly, identified with Fulgentius, bishop of Ruspe. The short On the Thebaid (Super Thebaiden) reads Statius’s epic as a moral allegory, extending the method of the Exposition of the Content of Virgil to Virgil’s successor. It survives in a single thirteenth-century manuscript, and Helm and Whitbread both doubt that it is by Fulgentius, Whitbread regarding it as the work of a later imitator. (Noted by Roger Pearse, 21 March 2009, and again on 13 July 2015.)
