Annals

No public-domain English translation of this work is available. The complete work was translated by Harry J. Magoulias as O City of Byzantium: Annals of Niketas Choniatēs (Byzantine Texts in Translation; Wayne State University Press, Detroit, 1984; ISBN 978-0-8143-1764-8), the first English translation; it is available from the publisher. Niketas Choniates (c. 1155–1217), a senior official at the imperial court, wrote his History (or Annals) in Greek as a narrative of the Byzantine Empire from the death of Alexios I Komnenos in 1118 down to about 1207. He was an eyewitness of its last chapters, and his account of the capture and sack of Constantinople by the Fourth Crusade in 1204 describes the looting of churches and of the imperial tombs in the Church of the Holy Apostles, and the melting down of the city’s ancient bronze statues for coin. (Noted by Roger Pearse, 14 December 2013; see also his post of the same day, “Melting down the statues in Constantinople in 1204”.)
