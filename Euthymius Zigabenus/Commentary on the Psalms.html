Commentary on the Psalms

No public-domain English translation of this work is available. A complete English translation by John Raffan, made from his own new edition of the Greek, was posted free online in 2016 on his Academia.edu page (a draft of the Introduction and Psalms 1–75 had appeared there in 2015): the English text and a parallel Greek and English text (downloading requires a free Academia.edu account). Its licence is not stated. Raffan’s edition is the first complete one: the text reprinted in the Patrologia Graeca, vol. 128, rests on a single manuscript with gaps and interpolations and lacks the commentary on the Biblical Canticles, whereas his is based chiefly on the twelfth-century Moscow Synodal manuscript gr. 195, collated with early manuscripts in Paris, London, Constantinople (the Old Seraglio library), Sinai, Florence and Munich. Euthymius Zigabenus (died after 1118) was a monk of the monastery of the Virgin near Constantinople, a favourite of the emperor Alexius I Comnenus and author of the Dogmatic Panoply against the heresies. His commentary covers the whole Psalter, drawing mainly on the earlier Greek Fathers, and closes with the Biblical Canticles. (Noted by Roger Pearse, 30 November 2015, and again on 9 June 2016, when the translation was completed.)
