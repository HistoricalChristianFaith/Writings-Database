Old English Life of Saint Giles

No public-domain English translation of this work is available. The only English translation, by Elaine Treharne, is in The Old English Life of St Nicholas with the Old English Life of St Giles (Leeds Texts and Monographs, New Series 15; Leeds Studies in English, University of Leeds, 1997; ISBN 978-0-902296-25-1), a critical edition of the Old English text with a modern English translation and commentary, printed together with a Latin text of its source; it is available from the publisher. It covers the complete Life; the main part of the volume is given to the Old English Life of St Nicholas. The Old English Life of St Giles (Egidius), the hermit and abbot of Provence, is an anonymous prose translation of the widely read Latin Vita Sancti Egidii, keeping close to its source; its only copy is in a mid-twelfth-century homiliary from Canterbury or Rochester, Cambridge, Corpus Christi College, MS 303, and Treharne argues that it was made in the late eleventh century by the same writer as the Old English Life of St Nicholas in that manuscript. (Noted by Roger Pearse, 26 January 2023.)
