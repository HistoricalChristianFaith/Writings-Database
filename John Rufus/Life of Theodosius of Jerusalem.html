Life of Theodosius of Jerusalem

No public-domain English translation of this work is available. The first English translation is by Cornelia B. Horn and Robert R. Phenix Jr., John Rufus: The Lives of Peter the Iberian, Theodosius of Jerusalem, and the Monk Romanus (Writings from the Greco-Roman World 24; Society of Biblical Literature, Atlanta, 2008; ISBN 978-1-58983-200-8; also issued in hardback by Brill, ISBN 978-90-04-14686-0), available from the publisher. The volume gives a new critical edition of the Syriac text with an English translation, an introduction and notes; its main text is the Life of Peter the Iberian, and it also contains the account of the monk Romanus. This short work, also known as the Commemoration of the Death of Theodosius (De obitu Theodosii), is anonymous but is generally attributed to John Rufus; written in Greek, it survives only in Syriac. It concerns Theodosius, the monk who led the Palestinian opposition to the Council of Chalcedon, held the see of Jerusalem in place of Juvenal from 451 to 453, and died in 457. (Noted by Roger Pearse, 10 November 2015.)
