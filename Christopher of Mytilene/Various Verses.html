Various Verses

No public-domain English translation of this work is available. The complete collection was edited and translated by Floris Bernard and Christopher Livanos in The Poems of Christopher of Mytilene and John Mauropous (Dumbarton Oaks Medieval Library 50; Harvard University Press, 2018; ISBN 978-0-674-73698-6), with the Greek text and a facing English translation, an introduction and notes; it is available from the publisher. The same volume also contains the poetry collection of John Mauropous. Christopher of Mytilene (c. 1000 – after 1050) was a high official and judge at Constantinople, and his Various Verses is a collection of 145 poems, mostly in twelve-syllable verse: satirical pieces mocking charioteers, false monks and other figures of the city, religious epigrams, epitaphs and laments for members of his family, riddles, dedicatory and occasional verses on persons and events of his day, among them poem 22, on the gout of Demetrius, Metropolitan of Cyzicus. The whole collection survives only in a single damaged thirteenth-century manuscript at Grottaferrata, so many poems have lost alternate lines. The earlier Greek edition by Eduard Kurtz, Die Gedichte des Christophoros Mytilenaios (Leipzig, 1903), is available at the Internet Archive. (Noted by Roger Pearse, 7 October 2026.)
