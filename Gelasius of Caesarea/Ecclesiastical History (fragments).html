Ecclesiastical History (fragments)

No public-domain English translation of this work is available. The only English translation, by Nicholas Marinides, is in Gelasius of Caesarea, Ecclesiastical History: The Extant Fragments, with an Appendix containing the Fragments from Dogmatic Writings, edited by Martin Wallraff, Jonathan Stutz and Nicholas Marinides (Die griechischen christlichen Schriftsteller der ersten Jahrhunderte, Neue Folge 25; De Gruyter, Berlin and Boston, 2018; ISBN 978-3-11-047580-7), which prints the testimonia and fragments with facing English translation after a long introduction; it is available from the publisher. It covers everything that survives of the work: the History itself is lost, and the edition is the first systematic collection of the passages that can be traced back to it. Gelasius, bishop of Caesarea in Palestine (died c. 395) and nephew of Cyril of Jerusalem, wrote a continuation of Eusebius’ Church History covering the fourth century. Later church historians and hagiographers, Rufinus of Aquileia among them, drew on it heavily, usually without naming him, and the fragments have to be recovered from their works. (Noted by Roger Pearse, 17 March 2018.)
