Sermon 25 On the Feast of the Nativity, 5

No public-domain English translation of this work is available. A complete English translation by Fr. Joseph Bolin, “Christmas Sermon 25 of Leo the Great” (December 2009), is free to read on his Paths of Love blog; the Latin edition it was made from is not named, and its licence is not stated. The sermon, one of the ten that Leo preached at Christmas and not among those translated in the Nicene and Post-Nicene Fathers, is accepted as authentic. In six short chapters Leo admits that the mystery of the Nativity exceeds what any preacher can say, and teaches that the eternal Son, born of the Virgin, took on human weakness without losing his divine glory; that he is truly God, eternally begotten of the Father, and truly man, born in time, against those who deny his eternal generation; that what the prophets glimpsed is fulfilled in the Incarnation, which worldly wisdom counts as folly; and that Christ, born without sin, undid Adam’s disobedience by his own obedience, so that his humility is both the means of our salvation and the example Christians are to follow. Other English translations: Jane Patricia Freeland and Agnes Josephine Conway, St. Leo the Great: Sermons (The Fathers of the Church 93; Catholic University of America Press, 1996; ISBN 978-0-8132-2829-7), the complete sermons, translated from Chavasse’s critical edition (CCSL 138–138A). (Noted by Roger Pearse, 5 December 2024.)
