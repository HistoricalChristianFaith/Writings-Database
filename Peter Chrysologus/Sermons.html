Sermons

No public-domain English translation of this work is available. All the sermons now accepted as genuine are translated in three volumes of The Fathers of the Church (Catholic University of America Press): George E. Ganss, Saint Peter Chrysologus: Selected Sermons, and Saint Valerian: Homilies (The Fathers of the Church 17, 1953; its US copyright was renewed in 1981; reprinted 2004, ISBN 978-0-8132-1389-7), about a third of the genuine sermons, available from the publisher; and William B. Palardy, St. Peter Chrysologus: Selected Sermons, Volume 2 (The Fathers of the Church 109, 2004; ISBN 978-0-8132-0109-2), which completes the genuine sermons up to number 72, and Volume 3 (The Fathers of the Church 110, 2005; ISBN 978-0-8132-0110-8), which completes the rest, available from the publisher (Volume 2, Volume 3). Peter, bishop of Ravenna from about 433 to 450, while the western imperial court resided there, is known almost entirely through these short sermons, which survive chiefly in the collection of 176 made by Bishop Felix of Ravenna in the eighth century. Most expound passages of the Gospels read through the church year; others treat St. Paul’s letters, the Creed and the Lord’s Prayer for those preparing for baptism, Lent, Easter, Christmas and Epiphany, feasts of saints and the consecration of bishops, and one pair attacks the half-pagan processions with which Ravenna kept the Kalends of January. They combine colloquial speech with vivid rhetoric and give a lively picture of Christian life in fifth-century Ravenna. (Noted by Roger Pearse, 1 January 2025.)
