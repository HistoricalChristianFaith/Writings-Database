On the Incarnation of Christ

No public-domain English translation of this work is available. The complete homily was edited and translated by Remco F. Regtuit, Severian of Gabala: Homily on the Incarnation of Christ (CPG 4204): Text, Translation and Introduction (Amsterdam: VU University Press, 1992; ISBN 978-90-5383-121-2); no publisher’s page for it could be found, but it is listed on Google Books. The book gives the Greek text of this single sermon (CPG 4204), one of the many works of Severian, bishop of Gabala in Syria and a popular preacher at Constantinople around 400, that have come down under the name of his rival John Chrysostom, together with an English translation and an introduction. (Noted by Roger Pearse, 26 June 2014.)
