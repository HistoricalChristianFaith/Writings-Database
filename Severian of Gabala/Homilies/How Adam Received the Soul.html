How Adam Received the Soul

No public-domain English translation of this work is available. A complete translation by Robert C. Hill is included in Commentaries on Genesis 1–3, by Severian of Gabala and Bede the Venerable, edited by Michael Glerup (Ancient Christian Texts; IVP Academic, 2010; ISBN 978-0-8308-2907-1), available from the publisher; the volume contains Severian’s six homilies on the creation (In cosmogoniam) followed by this one, and a translation of Book 1 of Bede’s commentary on Genesis by Carmen S. Hardin. The sermon (Quomodo animam acceperit Adam), on the creation of man and the giving of his soul, survives only in Henry Savile’s Eton edition of the works of John Chrysostom (1613), under whose name it was printed; it may have been preached straight after the six homilies on creation, in Lent 401. (Noted by Roger Pearse, 26 June 2014.)
