On the Sacrifices of Cain

No public-domain English translation of this work is available. A draft English translation, “Severian of Gabala, De Sacrificiis Caini (PG 62: 719-722 = CPG 4208): Draft Translation”, with the Greek and Latin text from Migne in parallel, was posted free online at Academia.edu in 2015 (Wayback Machine copy); the translator is not named in the notice, and its licence is not stated. The short sermon De sacrificiis Caini, on the offerings of Cain and Abel (Genesis 4), is printed by Migne among the spurious works of John Chrysostom (Patrologia Graeca 62, cols. 719–722) and is listed under Severian of Gabala in the Clavis Patrum Graecorum (CPG 4208), though the attribution is uncertain. (Noted by Roger Pearse, 17 June 2015.)
