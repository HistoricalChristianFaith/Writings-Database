On the Inscriptions of the Psalms

No public-domain English translation of this work is available. Richard (Casimir) McCambly’s translation, Saint Gregory of Nyssa: Commentary on the Inscriptions of the Psalms (Archbishop Iakovos Library of Ecclesiastical and Historical Sources 17; Hellenic College Press, Brookline, Mass.; ISBN 0-917653-45-9), is reproduced free, by permission of the publisher, as a PDF among his translations of Gregory of Nyssa on the Lectio Divina website; no licence for further use is stated. The treatise studies the Psalter as a guide to the soul’s ascent to blessedness. Its first part explains how the order of the Psalms, divided into five books, marks successive stages of that ascent; the second interprets the headings of the individual Psalms in the Greek Bible in the same spiritual sense. Other English translations: Ronald E. Heine, Gregory of Nyssa’s Treatise on the Inscriptions of the Psalms (Oxford Early Christian Studies; Clarendon Press, 1995; ISBN 978-0-19-826763-8), available from the publisher.
