Against Fate

No public-domain English translation of this work is available. An English translation of the Contra fatum by Richard (Casimir) McCambly, first published in the Greek Orthodox Theological Review 37.1–2 (1992), is offered free, with his introduction and notes, as a PDF among his translations of Gregory of Nyssa on the Lectio Divina website; its licence is not stated. Written as a letter reporting a debate Gregory held at Constantinople with a pagan philosopher, the work defends human freedom against astrological fatalism. To the philosopher’s claim that the position of the stars at a person’s birth fixes the course of his life, Gregory replies that such a fate would make virtue and vice meaningless and would deny the providence of God. (Noted by Roger Pearse, 10 May 2019.)
