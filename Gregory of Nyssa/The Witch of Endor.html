The Witch of Endor

No public-domain English translation of this work is available. An English translation of the De pythonissa by Richard (Casimir) McCambly, first published in the Greek Orthodox Theological Review 35.2 (1990), is offered free, with his introduction and notes, as a PDF among his translations of Gregory of Nyssa on the Lectio Divina website; its licence is not stated. In this letter to a fellow bishop, Theodosius, Gregory discusses the story of Saul and the medium of Endor (1 Samuel 28). Against Origen’s view that the woman really called up the soul of Samuel, he argues that the dead saint could not be summoned by sorcery, and that a demon took on Samuel’s appearance and deceived Saul with a forged prophecy. Other English translations: Rowan A. Greer and Margaret M. Mitchell, in The “Belly-Myther” of Endor: Interpretations of 1 Kingdoms 28 in the Early Church (Writings from the Greco-Roman World 16; Society of Biblical Literature, 2007; ISBN 978-1-58983-120-9), with the Greek text, available from the publisher. (Noted by Roger Pearse, 10 May 2019.)
