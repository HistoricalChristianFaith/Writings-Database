Two Homilies on Saint Stephen

No public-domain English translation of this work is available. An English translation of the In sanctum Stephanum I and II by Richard (Casimir) McCambly is offered free, with his introduction and notes, as a PDF among his translations of Gregory of Nyssa on the Lectio Divina website; its licence is not stated. The two sermons were preached for the feast of Stephen the protomartyr on the days after Christmas. The first sets Stephen’s departure from this life beside Christ’s entry into it, tells the story of his ministry and martyrdom from Acts, and uses his vision of Christ at the right hand of the Father against those who denied the divinity of the Son and the Spirit; the second, much shorter, passes from Stephen to the apostles Peter, James and John, whose feasts followed his. (Noted by Roger Pearse, 10 May 2019.)
