Against Those Who Practice Usury

No public-domain English translation of this work is available. An English translation of the Contra usurarios by Richard (Casimir) McCambly, first published in the Greek Orthodox Theological Review 36.3–4 (1991), is offered free, with his introduction and notes, as a PDF among his translations of Gregory of Nyssa on the Lectio Divina website; its licence is not stated. In this homily, which defers to his brother Basil’s sermon on the same subject, Gregory condemns lending money at interest as profiting from a neighbour’s need. He portrays the greed of the moneylender and the ruin of debtors and their families, and urges his hearers to give and lend without asking anything in return. (Noted by Roger Pearse, 10 May 2019.)
