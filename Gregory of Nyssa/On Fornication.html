On Fornication

No public-domain English translation of this work is available. An English translation of the Contra fornicarios by Richard (Casimir) McCambly is offered free, with his introduction and notes, as a PDF among his translations of Gregory of Nyssa on the Lectio Divina website; its licence is not stated. In this short homily Gregory expounds Paul’s warning “Flee fornication. Every sin that a man doeth is without the body; but he that committeth fornication sinneth against his own body” (1 Corinthians 6:18), explaining why sexual sin, unlike other sins, defiles the very body that is the temple of the Holy Spirit, and exhorting his hearers to purity. (Noted by Roger Pearse, 10 May 2019.)
