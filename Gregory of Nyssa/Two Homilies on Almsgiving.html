Two Homilies on Almsgiving

No public-domain English translation of this work is available. An English translation of the De beneficentia and In illud: Quatenus uni ex his fecistis by Richard (Casimir) McCambly is offered free, with his introduction and notes, as a PDF among his translations of Gregory of Nyssa on the Lectio Divina website; its licence is not stated. The two sermons, often called On the Love of the Poor 1 and 2, share the theme of kindness to the destitute. The first, preached in Lent, tells its hearers that fasting is worthless without mercy and urges them to share with the beggars and homeless who filled the towns; the second, on the words “inasmuch as ye have done it unto one of the least of these my brethren, ye have done it unto me” (Matthew 25:40), describes the misery of lepers driven out of society and calls on Christians to care for them as for Christ himself. Other English translations: Susan R. Holman, as an appendix to The Hungry Are Dying: Beggars and Bishops in Roman Cappadocia (Oxford Studies in Historical Theology; Oxford University Press, 2001; ISBN 978-0-19-513912-9), available from the publisher. (Noted by Roger Pearse, 10 May 2019.)
