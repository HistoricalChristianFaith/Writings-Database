First Homily on the Forty Martyrs

No public-domain English translation of this work is available. An English translation of the In quadraginta martyres Ia and Ib by Richard (Casimir) McCambly is offered free, with his introduction and notes, as a PDF among his translations of Gregory of Nyssa on the Lectio Divina website; its licence is not stated. The homily, in two parts, celebrates the Forty Martyrs of Sebaste, soldiers who were left to die of exposure on a frozen lake rather than deny Christ. Gregory praises their unity and endurance and commends them to his hearers as intercessors and protectors. Other English translations: Johan Leemans, in Johan Leemans, Wendy Mayer, Pauline Allen and Boudewijn Dehandschutter, ‘Let us die that we may live’: Greek homilies on Christian Martyrs from Asia Minor, Palestine and Syria c.350–c.450 AD (Routledge, 2003; ISBN 978-0-415-24042-0), available from the publisher. (Noted by Roger Pearse, 10 May 2019.)
