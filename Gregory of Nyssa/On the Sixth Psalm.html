On the Sixth Psalm

No public-domain English translation of this work is available. An English translation of the In sextum Psalmum by Richard (Casimir) McCambly, first published in the Greek Orthodox Theological Review 32.1 (1987), is offered free, with his introduction and notes, as a PDF among his translations of Gregory of Nyssa on the Lectio Divina website; its licence is not stated. This short treatise explains the heading of Psalm 6, “for the end, among the hymns, for the eighth”. Gregory takes “the eighth” to mean the eighth day, the age to come that follows the seven-day cycle of this present time, and so reads the psalm’s plea for mercy as the repentance fitting for those who look ahead to the judgment. (Noted by Roger Pearse, 10 May 2019.)
