In Praise of Theodore the Great Martyr

No public-domain English translation of this work is available. An English translation of the De sancto Theodoro by Richard (Casimir) McCambly is offered free, with his introduction and notes, as a PDF among his translations of Gregory of Nyssa on the Lectio Divina website; its licence is not stated. Gregory preached this panegyric on the feast of Theodore the Recruit at the martyr’s shrine at Euchaita, near Amaseia, during the winter. He describes the church built over the relics and its paintings, tells how the young soldier refused to sacrifice, set fire to a pagan temple and was burnt alive, and asks for his intercession against the barbarian invaders threatening the region. Other English translations: Johan Leemans, in Johan Leemans, Wendy Mayer, Pauline Allen and Boudewijn Dehandschutter, ‘Let us die that we may live’: Greek homilies on Christian Martyrs from Asia Minor, Palestine and Syria c.350–c.450 AD (Routledge, 2003; ISBN 978-0-415-24042-0), available from the publisher. (Noted by Roger Pearse, 10 May 2019.)
