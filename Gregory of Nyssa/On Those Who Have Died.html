On Those Who Have Died

No public-domain English translation of this work is available. An English translation of the De mortuis by Richard (Casimir) McCambly is offered free, with his introduction and notes, as a PDF among his translations of Gregory of Nyssa on the Lectio Divina website; its licence is not stated. Taking its starting point from Paul’s words about “them which are asleep” (1 Thessalonians 4:13), the discourse argues that death should not be mourned as an evil: it frees the soul from the body’s passions, and through purification leads on to the resurrection, when human nature will be restored to the condition God first intended for it. (Noted by Roger Pearse, 10 May 2019.)
