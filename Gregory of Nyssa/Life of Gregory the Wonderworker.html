Life of Gregory the Wonderworker

No public-domain English translation of this work is available. An English translation of the De vita Gregorii Thaumaturgi by Richard (Casimir) McCambly is offered free, with his introduction and notes, as a PDF among his translations of Gregory of Nyssa on the Lectio Divina website; its licence is not stated. This panegyric on Gregory Thaumaturgus, the third-century bishop of Neocaesarea in Pontus and pupil of Origen, was probably preached on his feast day. Drawing on the traditions still told about him in Pontus, it recounts the saint’s education, the creed revealed to him in a vision, his miracles and his conversion of almost the whole of his pagan city, and is the main source for his life. Other English translations: Michael Slusser, in St. Gregory Thaumaturgus: Life and Works (The Fathers of the Church 98; Catholic University of America Press, 1998; ISBN 978-0-8132-0098-9), available from the publisher. (Noted by Roger Pearse, 10 May 2019.)
