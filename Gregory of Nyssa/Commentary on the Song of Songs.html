Commentary on the Song of Songs

No public-domain English translation of this work is available. Richard (Casimir) McCambly’s translation, Saint Gregory of Nyssa: Commentary on the Song of Songs (Archbishop Iakovos Library of Ecclesiastical and Historical Sources 12; Hellenic College Press, Brookline, Mass., 1987; ISBN 0-917653-18-1), is reproduced free, by permission of the publisher, as two PDFs on the Lectio Divina website among his translations of Gregory of Nyssa: part 1 (preface, introduction and Homilies 1–6) and part 2 (Homilies 7–15); no licence for further use is stated. The fifteen homilies, written late in Gregory’s life and dedicated to the deaconess Olympias of Constantinople, expound the Song of Songs from its opening verse to 6:9. Gregory reads the love of the bride and the bridegroom as the union of the soul, and of the Church, with God, and describes the soul’s progress as a never-ending ascent in which each attainment of the good awakens a desire for more. Other English translations: Richard A. Norris Jr., Gregory of Nyssa: Homilies on the Song of Songs (Writings from the Greco-Roman World 13; Society of Biblical Literature, 2012; ISBN 978-1-58983-105-6), with the Greek text, available from the publisher.
