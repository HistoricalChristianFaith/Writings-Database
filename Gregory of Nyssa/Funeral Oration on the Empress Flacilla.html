Funeral Oration on the Empress Flacilla

No public-domain English translation of this work is available. An English translation of the Oratio funebris in Flacillam imperatricem by Richard (Casimir) McCambly is offered free, with his introduction and notes, as a PDF among his translations of Gregory of Nyssa on the Lectio Divina website; its licence is not stated. Gregory delivered this funeral oration at Constantinople for Aelia Flacilla, wife of the emperor Theodosius I, a few months after the death of her young daughter Pulcheria; he praises her humility, her care for the poor and her loyalty to the Nicene faith against the Arians, and consoles the court and the city with the hope of the resurrection. (Noted by Roger Pearse, 10 May 2019.)
