Hexaemeron

No public-domain English translation of this work is available. An English translation of the In Hexaemeron by Richard (Casimir) McCambly is offered free, with his introduction and notes, as a PDF among his translations of Gregory of Nyssa on the Lectio Divina website; its licence is not stated. Written for Gregory’s brother Peter, bishop of Sebaste, soon after the death of their brother Basil, the treatise defends and completes Basil’s homilies on the six days of creation. Gregory answers the difficulties critics found in the order of the account in Genesis 1, such as light existing before the sun, by explaining that God created everything at once in its potential, and that the “days” describe the necessary sequence in which the world then unfolded. Other English translations: Robin Orton, On the Six Days of Creation (Fathers of the Church Shorter Works; Catholic University of America Press, 2021; ISBN 978-0-8132-3376-5), available from the publisher; and Andrew Radde-Gallwitz, in Johannes Zachhuber and Anna Marmodoro (eds.), Gregory of Nyssa: On the Hexaemeron. Text, Translation, and Essays (Oxford University Press, 2025; ISBN 978-0-19-889708-8), with the Greek text. (Noted by Roger Pearse, 10 May 2019.)
