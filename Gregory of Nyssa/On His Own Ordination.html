On His Own Ordination

No public-domain English translation of this work is available. An English translation of the In suam ordinationem by Richard (Casimir) McCambly is offered free, with his introduction and notes, as a PDF among his translations of Gregory of Nyssa on the Lectio Divina website; its licence is not stated. The oration, also known as On the Divinity, against Evagrius (De deitate adversus Evagrium), was delivered before an assembly of bishops. After excusing his own ageing and halting voice beside the speakers who had gone before him, Gregory turns to the Holy Spirit and defends the Spirit’s divinity against the Pneumatomachians, who denied it. (Noted by Roger Pearse, 10 May 2019.)
