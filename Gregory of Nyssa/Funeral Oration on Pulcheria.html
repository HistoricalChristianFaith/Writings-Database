Funeral Oration on Pulcheria

No public-domain English translation of this work is available. An English translation of the Consolatoria in Pulcheriam by Richard (Casimir) McCambly is offered free, with his introduction and notes, as a PDF among his translations of Gregory of Nyssa on the Lectio Divina website; its licence is not stated. This consolatory oration was preached at Constantinople after the death of Pulcheria, the small daughter of the emperor Theodosius I and the empress Flacilla. Gregory gives full voice to the grief of the city and the imperial family before turning to the consolations of Christian hope in the resurrection. (Noted by Roger Pearse, 10 May 2019.)
