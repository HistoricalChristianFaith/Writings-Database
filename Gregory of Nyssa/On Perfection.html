On Perfection

No public-domain English translation of this work is available. An English translation of the De perfectione by Richard (Casimir) McCambly, first published in the Greek Orthodox Theological Review 29.4 (1984), is offered free, with his introduction and notes, as a PDF among his translations of Gregory of Nyssa on the Lectio Divina website; its licence is not stated. The treatise is a letter to the monk Olympius, who had asked how one might attain perfection. Gregory answers by drawing out the meaning of the many names that Paul gives to Christ, such as power, wisdom, peace, light and image of the invisible God: since a Christian bears Christ’s name, perfection lies in imitating what each name signifies, a growth that never comes to an end. Other English translations: Virginia Woods Callahan, in Saint Gregory of Nyssa: Ascetical Works (The Fathers of the Church 58; Catholic University of America Press, 1967; ISBN 978-0-8132-0969-2), available from the publisher. (Noted by Roger Pearse, 10 May 2019.)
