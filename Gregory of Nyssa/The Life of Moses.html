The Life of Moses

No public-domain English translation of this work is available. The complete work was translated by Abraham J. Malherbe and Everett Ferguson as Gregory of Nyssa: The Life of Moses (The Classics of Western Spirituality; Paulist Press, 1978; ISBN 978-0-8091-2112-0), with an introduction and notes and a preface by John Meyendorff; it is available from the publisher. Written late in Gregory’s life for a younger friend who had asked for guidance on the perfect life, the treatise has two books: the first retells the story of Moses from Exodus and Numbers, and the second contemplates its spiritual meaning, reading each episode, from the burning bush and the crossing of the sea to the ascent of Sinai into the darkness where God was, as a stage in the soul’s unending progress towards God. (Noted by Roger Pearse, 17 August 2023.)
