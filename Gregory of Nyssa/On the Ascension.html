On the Ascension

No public-domain English translation of this work is available. An English translation of the In ascensionem Christi by Richard (Casimir) McCambly is offered free, with his introduction and notes, as a PDF among his translations of Gregory of Nyssa on the Lectio Divina website; its licence is not stated. This short festal sermon is among the earliest witnesses to a feast of the Ascension kept separately from Pentecost. Rather than retelling the event, Gregory expounds Psalms 23 and 24 (22 and 23 in the Greek), reading the Good Shepherd psalm as a guide for those approaching baptism and the cry “Lift up your heads, O ye gates” as the angels’ welcome to Christ returning to heaven. (Noted by Roger Pearse, 10 May 2019.)
