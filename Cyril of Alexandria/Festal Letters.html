Festal Letters

No public-domain English translation of this work is available. The complete collection is translated by Philip R. Amidon, S.J., edited by John J. O’Keefe, in two volumes of The Fathers of the Church (Catholic University of America Press): Festal Letters 1–12 (FOC 118, 2009; ISBN 978-0-8132-0118-4) and Festal Letters 13–30 (FOC 127, 2013; ISBN 978-0-8132-2184-7). Each year the bishop of Alexandria sent the churches of Egypt a pastoral letter announcing the dates of Lent and Easter, and usually adding moral exhortation and doctrinal teaching. Cyril’s festal letters cover almost all of his years as bishop (412–444); they are a view of his work as a pastor rather than as a controversialist, though they also take up arguments against pagans, Jews and heretics. (Noted by Roger Pearse, 16 June 2009; see also his post of 25 May 2015.)
