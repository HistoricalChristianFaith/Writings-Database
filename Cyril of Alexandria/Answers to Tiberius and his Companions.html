Answers to Tiberius and his Companions

No public-domain English translation of this work is available. The complete text is translated by Lionel R. Wickham in Cyril of Alexandria: Select Letters (Oxford Early Christian Texts; Clarendon Press, Oxford, 1983; ISBN 978-0-19-826810-9), pp. 132–179, with the Greek text on facing pages and an introduction; it is available from the publisher. The volume is a selection of ten pieces; besides the Answers it includes the Doctrinal Questions and Answers, the second and third letters to Nestorius, and other doctrinal letters. The Answers to Tiberius and his Companions (Responsiones ad Tiberium diaconum sociosque suos) are Cyril’s replies to a set of questions on points of doctrine and the interpretation of Scripture that were brought to him by the deacon Tiberius and his fellow monks from Palestine. They come from a monastic setting rather than from the Nestorian controversy, and were handed down apart from the collections of conciliar letters. (Noted by Roger Pearse, 13 May 2010.)
