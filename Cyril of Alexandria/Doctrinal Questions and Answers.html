Doctrinal Questions and Answers

No public-domain English translation of this work is available. The complete text is translated by Lionel R. Wickham in Cyril of Alexandria: Select Letters (Oxford Early Christian Texts; Clarendon Press, Oxford, 1983; ISBN 978-0-19-826810-9), pp. 180–213, with the Greek text on facing pages and an introduction; it is available from the publisher. The volume is a selection of ten pieces; besides this work it includes the Answers to Tiberius and his Companions, the second and third letters to Nestorius, and other doctrinal letters. The Doctrinal Questions and Answers (Solutiones) are a short collection of Cyril’s answers to questions on points of doctrine and Scripture. Like the Answers to Tiberius, they come from a monastic setting rather than from the Nestorian controversy, and were handed down apart from the collections of conciliar letters. (Noted by Roger Pearse, 13 May 2010.)
