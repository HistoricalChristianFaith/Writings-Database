Letters

No public-domain English translation of this work is available. The complete collection is translated by John I. McEnerney in two volumes of The Fathers of the Church (Catholic University of America Press, 1987): Letters 1–50 (FOC 76; ISBN 978-0-8132-0076-7) and Letters 51–110 (FOC 77; ISBN 978-0-8132-0077-4). The collection of 110 pieces includes letters written to Cyril as well as his own, among them letters from Nestorius, Pope Celestine and John of Antioch. Most of it concerns the controversy with Nestorius over the title Theotokos (“Mother of God”), the Council of Ephesus in 431, and the reconciliation with John of Antioch and the Eastern bishops in 433; later letters also take up the writings of Diodore of Tarsus and Theodore of Mopsuestia, church discipline and other matters. A selection of seven doctrinal letters (Letters 4, 17, 41, 44, 45, 46 and 55) is also translated, with the Greek text, by Lionel R. Wickham in Cyril of Alexandria: Select Letters (Oxford Early Christian Texts; Clarendon Press, 1983; ISBN 978-0-19-826810-9). Three letters are hosted on this site in the public-domain translation of the Nicene and Post-Nicene Fathers (Second Series, vol. 14): the second and third letters to Nestorius (Letters 4 and 17), under the Council of Ephesus, as “The Epistle of Cyril to Nestorius” and “The Epistle of Cyril to Nestorius with the 12 Anathematisms”, and the letter to John of Antioch (Letter 39), under the Council of Chalcedon.
