Commentary on Genesis

No public-domain English translation of this work is available. The first English translation, by Deirdre Dempsey, Safwat Marzouk and Stephen J. Davis, is Ibn al-Ṭayyib's Commentary on Genesis (Fordham University Press, 2026; ISBN 978-1-5315-1483-9), with a critical introduction to his life, exegetical method, and theology of creation; it is available from the publisher. The commentary is the first part of The Paradise of Christianity, the complete Bible commentary of Abū-l-Faraj ibn al-Ṭayyib (d. 1043), a Baghdad priest, physician and philosopher of the Church of the East. (Noted by Roger Pearse, 12 November 2026.)
