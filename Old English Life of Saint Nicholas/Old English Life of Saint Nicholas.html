Old English Life of Saint Nicholas

No public-domain English translation of this work is available. The only English translation, by Elaine Treharne, is in The Old English Life of St Nicholas with the Old English Life of St Giles (Leeds Texts and Monographs, New Series 15; Leeds Studies in English, University of Leeds, 1997; ISBN 978-0-902296-25-1), a critical edition of the Old English text with a modern English translation and commentary; it is available from the publisher. It covers the complete Life, and an appendix prints a Latin text of its source, the Life of Nicholas by John the Deacon of Naples, transcribed from a Cotton manuscript. The same volume also edits and translates the Old English Life of St Giles. The Old English Life of St Nicholas is an anonymous prose translation, made probably in the late eleventh century, of John the Deacon’s ninth-century Latin Life, beginning with John’s own prologue addressed to his “father Anastasius”; it survives in a twelfth-century homiliary from the south-east of England, Cambridge, Corpus Christi College, MS 303, and Treharne argues that it is by the same writer as the Old English Life of St Giles in that manuscript. (Noted by Roger Pearse, 26 January 2023, who also points to a review by Bengt Lindström that lists many corrections to the translation.)
