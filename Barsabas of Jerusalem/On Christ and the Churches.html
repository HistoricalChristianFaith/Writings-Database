On Christ and the Churches

No public-domain English translation of this work is available. A draft text with a complete English translation by David P. George, “Barsabas of Jerusalem, On Christ and the Churches (CPG 1685): Text and Translation”, is freely available on Academia.edu; its licence is not stated. The work was first published, with a French translation, by Michel van Esbroeck in Patrologia Orientalis 41 (1982), pp. 151–256. It survives only in a Georgian version, itself translated from a lost Greek original, in a single manuscript on Mount Athos (Iviron 11), under the title “The Word of Saint Barsabas, Archbishop of Jerusalem, about our Saviour Jesus Christ and the Churches [and about the High Priests]”. It is a homily that reads a series of Old Testament figures and events as types foreshadowing Christ and the Church. Van Esbroeck judged its theology to be early and dated it to the second or third century, written probably at Jerusalem, though not by any known archbishop of that city; Dmitry F. Bumazhnov discusses its date and its treatment of the Jews in Scrinium 4 (2008), pp. 121–135. (Noted by Roger Pearse, 5 November 2025, who suspects that the work’s use of the word “Trinity” is a later gloss.)
