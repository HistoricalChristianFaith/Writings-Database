To the Church (Ad Ecclesiam)

No public-domain English translation of this work is available. The complete work was translated by Jeremiah F. O'Sullivan in The Writings of Salvian, the Presbyter (The Fathers of the Church 3; New York: Fathers of the Church, Inc., 1947), which also contains The Governance of God and Salvian's nine letters; the volume is now published by the Catholic University of America Press (paperback, 2008; ISBN 978-0-8132-1550-1) and is available from the publisher. Written around 440, before The Governance of God, which quotes it, this treatise in four books is addressed “to the Catholic Church spread throughout the world” in the manner of an apostolic letter and was put out under the name of “Timothy” rather than Salvian's own. It is an attack on avarice among Christians, laity, clergy and those vowed to the religious life alike, and above all on the habit of leaving one's wealth to heirs and worldly men instead of giving it to the poor and to the Church for the good of one's own soul. Salvian's reasons for the pseudonym are given in his Letter 9, to Salonius, which is prefixed to the treatise in the manuscripts. (Noted by Roger Pearse, 12 October 2013.)
