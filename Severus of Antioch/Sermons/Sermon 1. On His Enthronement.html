Sermon 1. On His Enthronement

No public-domain English translation of this work is available. An English translation of Cathedral Homily 1 of Severus of Antioch, posted without a translator’s name on the St George Orthodox Ministry website (the mission site of Father Peter Farrington of the Coptic Orthodox Church in the UK) on 2 February 2021, is available free online: Homily 1. Its licence is not stated. Severus, patriarch of Antioch from 512 to 518 (d. 538), preached his 125 Cathedral Homilies (Homiliae cathedrales) in Antioch during his patriarchate; they are preserved complete only in Syriac (in a version later revised by Jacob of Edessa). Homily 1 is his first homily as patriarch, given in the martyrium of St Romanus: he tells how he came to be raised to the see of Antioch, and, against Eutyches, insists on confessing Christ “of two natures”, one Lord and one Son, while rejecting two natures after the union. The posted version marks a few small omissions. (The St George Ministry translations were noted by Roger Pearse, 17 March 2018.)
