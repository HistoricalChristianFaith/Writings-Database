Sermon 122. On the Gift of Linen for the Sick

No public-domain English translation of this work is available. An English translation of Cathedral Homily 122 of Severus of Antioch, posted without a translator’s name on the St George Orthodox Ministry website (the mission site of Father Peter Farrington of the Coptic Orthodox Church in the UK) on 6 October 2022, is available free online: Homily 122. Its licence is not stated. Severus, patriarch of Antioch from 512 to 518 (d. 538), preached his 125 Cathedral Homilies (Homiliae cathedrales) in Antioch during his patriarchate; they are preserved complete only in Syriac (in a version later revised by Jacob of Edessa). Homily 122 is an exhortation to bring the customary gift of linen for those suffering from leprosy or other illness, recalling his earlier sermon on the man who went down from Jerusalem to Jericho. The posted version gives extracts, with a short introduction by the translator. (The St George Ministry translations were noted by Roger Pearse, 17 March 2018.)
