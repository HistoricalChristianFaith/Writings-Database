Sermon 89. On the Man Who Fell Among Brigands

No public-domain English translation of this work is available. An English translation of Cathedral Homily 89 of Severus of Antioch, posted without a translator’s name on the St George Orthodox Ministry website (the mission site of Father Peter Farrington of the Coptic Orthodox Church in the UK) on 4 November 2021, is available free online: Homily 89. Its licence is not stated. Severus, patriarch of Antioch from 512 to 518 (d. 538), preached his 125 Cathedral Homilies (Homiliae cathedrales) in Antioch during his patriarchate; they are preserved complete only in Syriac (in a version later revised by Jacob of Edessa). Homily 89 is a Lenten sermon expounding the parable of the Good Samaritan (Luke 10), and rebuking those who had not brought the customary strips of linen for people suffering from leprosy and other sores. (The St George Ministry translations were noted by Roger Pearse, 17 March 2018.)
