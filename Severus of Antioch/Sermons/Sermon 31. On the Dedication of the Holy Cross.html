Sermon 31. On the Dedication of the Holy Cross

No public-domain English translation of this work is available. An English translation of Cathedral Homily 31 of Severus of Antioch, posted without a translator’s name on the St George Orthodox Ministry website (the mission site of Father Peter Farrington of the Coptic Orthodox Church in the UK) on 23 March 2021, is available free online: Homily 31. Its licence is not stated. Severus, patriarch of Antioch from 512 to 518 (d. 538), preached his 125 Cathedral Homilies (Homiliae cathedrales) in Antioch during his patriarchate; they are preserved complete only in Syriac (in a version later revised by Jacob of Edessa). Homily 31 is a sermon for the feast of the dedication of the Holy Cross, preached also on the anniversary of an earthquake, comparing the feast with the dedication of Solomon’s temple and drawing on baptism as our crucifixion with Christ. (The St George Ministry translations were noted by Roger Pearse, 17 March 2018.)
