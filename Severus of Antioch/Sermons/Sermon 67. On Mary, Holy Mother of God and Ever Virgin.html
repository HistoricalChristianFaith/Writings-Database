Sermon 67. On Mary, Holy Mother of God and Ever Virgin

No public-domain English translation of this work is available. An English translation of Cathedral Homily 67 of Severus of Antioch, posted without a translator’s name on the St George Orthodox Ministry website (the mission site of Father Peter Farrington of the Coptic Orthodox Church in the UK) on 15 December 2017, is available free online: Homily 67. Its licence is not stated. Severus, patriarch of Antioch from 512 to 518 (d. 538), preached his 125 Cathedral Homilies (Homiliae cathedrales) in Antioch during his patriarchate; they are preserved complete only in Syriac (in a version later revised by Jacob of Edessa). Homily 67 is a sermon on the Virgin Mother of God and on the virginal conception and birth of God made flesh, approaching its subject through the “holy ground” of the burning bush, Jacob at Bethel (“the gate of heaven”) and the theophany on Sinai. Maurice Brière’s Patrologia Orientalis edition (vol. 8, fasc. 2, 1912) gives the Syriac with a French translation. (The St George Ministry translations were noted by Roger Pearse, 17 March 2018.)
