Sermon 10. On the Baptism of Christ

No public-domain English translation of this work is available. An English translation of Cathedral Homily 10 of Severus of Antioch, posted without a translator’s name on the St George Orthodox Ministry website (the mission site of Father Peter Farrington of the Coptic Orthodox Church in the UK) on 1 April 2021, is available free online: Homily 10. Its licence is not stated. Severus, patriarch of Antioch from 512 to 518 (d. 538), preached his 125 Cathedral Homilies (Homiliae cathedrales) in Antioch during his patriarchate; they are preserved complete only in Syriac (in a version later revised by Jacob of Edessa). Homily 10 is a feast-day sermon on Christ’s growth “in wisdom and in age” and his baptism in the Jordan, the descent of the Spirit and the Father’s voice, with arguments against Eutyches, the Manichees and Nestorius, ending in praise of John the Baptist and a call to fasting and almsgiving. (The St George Ministry translations were noted by Roger Pearse, 17 March 2018.)
