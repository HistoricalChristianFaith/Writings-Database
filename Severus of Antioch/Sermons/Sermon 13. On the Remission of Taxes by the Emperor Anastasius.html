Sermon 13. On the Remission of Taxes by the Emperor Anastasius

No public-domain English translation of this work is available. An English translation of Cathedral Homily 13 of Severus of Antioch, posted without a translator’s name on the St George Orthodox Ministry website (the mission site of Father Peter Farrington of the Coptic Orthodox Church in the UK) on 17 March 2021, is available free online: Homily 13. Its licence is not stated. Severus, patriarch of Antioch from 512 to 518 (d. 538), preached his 125 Cathedral Homilies (Homiliae cathedrales) in Antioch during his patriarchate; they are preserved complete only in Syriac (in a version later revised by Jacob of Edessa). Homily 13 is a sermon of thanks for the emperor Anastasius’ remission of arrears of taxes owed to the treasury, set against Christ, who as an unborn child in the Virgin’s womb was enrolled and taxed with his subjects. The posted version marks some gaps in the text. (The St George Ministry translations were noted by Roger Pearse, 17 March 2018.)
