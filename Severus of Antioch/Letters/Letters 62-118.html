Letters 62-118

Letters 62–118 (LXII–CXVIII) of the collection are not yet hosted here. They form the second part of E. W. Brooks’ edition and English translation, A Collection of Letters of Severus of Antioch from numerous Syriac manuscripts, II (Patrologia Orientalis XIV, fasc. 1; Paris: Firmin-Didot, 1920), which is public domain by date (published 1920). The printed volume, with the Syriac above the English on each page, can be read in a scan at the Internet Archive, and Roger Pearse’s transcription of the English (2003; “All material on this page is in the public domain - copy freely”) is online at tertullian.org. Like Letters 1–61, these are letters and fragments of letters of Severus that survive in Syriac outside the Athanasian “Sixth Book of Select Letters”, gathered by Brooks from catenae and other manuscripts; among them is the letter to Thomas, bishop of Germanicea (CVIII), on the Gospel accounts of the hour of the crucifixion and the piercing of Christ’s side, which cites Eusebius’ Gospel Problems and Solutions. The volume ends with an appendix giving a canon (a set of hymns) in honour of Severus for his commemoration on 8 February. (Noted by Roger Pearse, 24 February 2010; see also his post of 20 February 2010, which quotes the passage.)
