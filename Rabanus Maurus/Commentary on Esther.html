Commentary on Esther

No public-domain English translation of this work is available. The first English translation is in Saints of the Old Testament: Rabanus Maurus’ Commentary on Ruth, Judith, and Esther (Scriptorium Press, 2024; ISBN 9798870443119), one volume containing complete translations of all three of Rabanus’s commentaries, with an introduction on his life and exegesis and the short life of Rabanus by Johannes Trithemius; the translator is not named. It is available from the publisher, whose site also offers a free sample. The commentary on Esther, given there with its preface, dedicatory letter and poem and fourteen chapters covering the whole book, was written around 834 and sent, together with the commentary on Judith, to the Empress Judith, wife of Louis the Pious, as an example for her to follow. It explains the story of Esther, who saved her people from destruction, section by section, chiefly in an allegorical sense, drawing on earlier Fathers such as Augustine, Gregory the Great and Bede. (Noted by Roger Pearse, 3 December 2024.)
