Commentary on Ruth

No public-domain English translation of this work is available. The first English translation is in Saints of the Old Testament: Rabanus Maurus’ Commentary on Ruth, Judith, and Esther (Scriptorium Press, 2024; ISBN 9798870443119), one volume containing complete translations of all three of Rabanus’s commentaries, with an introduction on his life and exegesis and the short life of Rabanus by Johannes Trithemius; the translator is not named. It is available from the publisher, whose site also offers a free sample. The commentary on Ruth, given there as a preface and sixteen chapters running through the whole book, is a section-by-section allegorical exposition in which Rabanus, the ninth-century abbot of Fulda and archbishop of Mainz, gathers and arranges the interpretations of earlier writers such as Augustine, Gregory the Great and Bede, reading the story of Ruth, the foreign woman who joins Israel, as a figure of the Church. (Noted by Roger Pearse, 3 December 2024.)
