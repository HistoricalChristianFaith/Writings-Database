Apologetic Book for the Martyrs

No public-domain English translation of this work is available. A complete English translation of the Liber Apologeticus Martyrum by Aymenn Jawad Al-Tamimi, with commentary and notes, is free online on his website as “Eulogius’ Defence of the Martyrs of Córdoba” (22 July 2020, since revised). Its licence is not stated. Eulogius’s last work, written in the late 850s, defends the Christians executed at Córdoba under the Umayyad emirate against those, including many clergy, who denied that they were true martyrs because they had sought death and worked no miracles. It contains an early Latin account of the life of the Prophet, which Eulogius says he found in a book at Pamplona, and closes with the passion of the priest Rudericus (Roderick) and of Salomon, executed in 857. Other English translations: Kenneth Baxter Wolf, The Eulogius Corpus (Translated Texts for Historians 71; Liverpool University Press, 2019; ISBN 978-1-78962-079-5), a translation of all of Eulogius’s works together with Paul Alvarus’s Life of Eulogius, available from the publisher. (Noted by Roger Pearse, 16 June 2021.)
