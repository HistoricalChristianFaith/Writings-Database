Documentum Martyriale

No public-domain English translation of this work is available. A complete English translation by Aymenn Jawad Al-Tamimi, with an introduction and notes, is free online on his website (6 December 2020, since revised); it is made from the Latin text in Juan Gil’s Corpus Scriptorum Muzarabicorum (Madrid, 1973), checked against Lorenzana’s edition (Madrid, 1785). Its licence is not stated. The Documentum Martyriale (“Lesson of Martyrdom”) was written by Eulogius in prison at Córdoba in late 851 and addressed to the virgins Flora and Maria, then held for denouncing the Prophet (Flora, the daughter of a Muslim father, was also charged with apostasy). Drawing heavily on Scripture, it urges them not to retract their confession, warns them against the arguments of those who would persuade them to save their lives, and holds out the reward awaiting them with Christ the bridegroom; they were executed on 24 November 851. Other English translations: Kenneth Baxter Wolf, The Eulogius Corpus (Translated Texts for Historians 71; Liverpool University Press, 2019; ISBN 978-1-78962-079-5), a translation of all of Eulogius’s works together with Paul Alvarus’s Life of Eulogius, available from the publisher. (Noted by Roger Pearse, 16 June 2021.)
