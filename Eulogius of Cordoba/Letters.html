Letters

No public-domain English translation of these letters is available. A complete English translation by Aymenn Jawad Al-Tamimi, with an introduction and notes, is free online on his website (8 December 2020); it is made from the Latin text in Juan Gil’s Corpus Scriptorum Muzarabicorum (Madrid, 1973), checked against Lorenzana’s edition (Madrid, 1785). Its licence is not stated. The collection contains Eulogius’s letters to his friend Paul Alvarus asking for his opinion of the first book of the Memoriale Sanctorum and of the Documentum Martyriale, with Alvarus’s replies; his letter to Alvarus after his release from prison, telling of the martyrdom of Flora and Maria; his letter to Baldegotho, Flora’s sister, on the same; and his letter to Wiliesindus, bishop of Pamplona, recalling his travels in northern Spain and promising to send him relics of the martyrs. Other English translations: Kenneth Baxter Wolf, The Eulogius Corpus (Translated Texts for Historians 71; Liverpool University Press, 2019; ISBN 978-1-78962-079-5), a translation of all of Eulogius’s works together with Paul Alvarus’s Life of Eulogius, available from the publisher. (Noted by Roger Pearse, 16 June 2021.)
