Anonymous Collection

No public-domain English translation of this work is available. The complete collection was translated by John Wortley in The Anonymous Sayings of the Desert Fathers: A Select Edition and Complete English Translation (Cambridge University Press, 2013; ISBN 978-0-521-50988-6), which prints a Greek text of selected sayings opposite an English version of all of them; it is available from the publisher. A selection, arranged by subject, was translated by Benedicta Ward as The Wisdom of the Desert Fathers: Systematic Sayings from the Anonymous Series of the Apophthegmata Patrum (Fairacres Publication 48; Oxford: SLG Press, 1975; later editions, ISBN 978-0-7283-0109-2), available from the publisher. In the Greek manuscripts the alphabetical collection of the Sayings of the Desert Fathers, grouped under the names of the elders, is followed by a long series of sayings and stories whose speakers are not named (“an old man said”, “a brother asked an elder”). The first editor of the alphabetical collection, Cotelier (1677), worked from a manuscript that lacks this series, so it has been printed and translated as a separate work; only parts of the Greek have been edited, by François Nau in the Revue de l’Orient Chrétien (1905–1913). The sayings deal with the everyday struggles of the monastic life: staying in the cell, fasting and prayer, temptation and thoughts, humility, obedience and charity towards others.
