Systematic Collection

No public-domain English translation of this work is available. The complete Greek systematic collection was translated by John Wortley as The Book of the Elders: Sayings of the Desert Fathers: The Systematic Collection (Cistercian Studies 240; Collegeville, Minn.: Cistercian Publications / Liturgical Press, 2012; ISBN 978-0-87907-201-8; paperback 2018, ISBN 978-0-87907-690-0), with a foreword by Bernard Flusin; it is available from the publisher. Wortley translates the Greek text edited, with a French translation, by Jean-Claude Guy in Sources Chrétiennes 387, 474 and 498 (1993–2005). The systematic collection is the second of the two great Greek collections of the Sayings of the Desert Fathers. Instead of grouping the sayings under the names of the elders, it sorts them by subject into chapters on the monastic virtues and the struggles of the monk’s life, such as stillness, compunction, self-control, discretion, watchfulness, unceasing prayer, obedience, humility and love; within each chapter the named sayings come first, in the order of the Greek alphabet, followed by the anonymous ones. It was compiled in the sixth century and later revised; an early form of it lies behind the Latin translation of Pelagius and John (the Verba Seniorum).
