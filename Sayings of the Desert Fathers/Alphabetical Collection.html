Alphabetical Collection

No public-domain English translation of this work is available. The complete collection was translated by Benedicta Ward as The Sayings of the Desert Fathers: The Alphabetical Collection (Cistercian Studies 59; Kalamazoo: Cistercian Publications, and London: Mowbray, 1975; revised edition 1984; ISBN 978-0-87907-959-8), with a preface by Metropolitan Anthony Bloom; it is available from the publisher. Ward follows the only printed Greek text, Cotelier’s edition of 1677 (reprinted in the Patrologia Graeca, vol. 65, cols. 71–440), which was made from a single manuscript, Paris gr. 1599. A second complete translation is John Wortley, Give Me a Word: The Alphabetical Sayings of the Desert Fathers (Popular Patristics Series 52; St Vladimir’s Seminary Press, 2014; ISBN 978-0-88141-497-4), available from the publisher. The sayings of Abba Moses alone are also translated in the anthology Saints of Ethiopia (Scriptorium Press; ISBN 979-8-3009-0117-2), available from the publisher. The Apophthegmata Patrum, or Sayings of the Desert Fathers, are short sayings and anecdotes of the monks, hermits and a few nuns of the Egyptian desert from the fourth century onward, passed on by word of mouth before they were written down in Greek. In this collection they are grouped under the name of the elder to whom each is credited, and the elders are arranged in the order of the Greek alphabet, beginning with Antony the Great; the manuscripts follow these named sayings with a series of anonymous ones, which have been edited and translated separately as the Anonymous Collection.
