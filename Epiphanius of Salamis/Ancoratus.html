Ancoratus

No public-domain English translation of this work is available. The complete work was translated by Young Richard Kim as St. Epiphanius of Cyprus: Ancoratus (The Fathers of the Church 128; Catholic University of America Press, 2014; ISBN 978-0-8132-2591-3), with an introduction; the volume also includes the two letters, from the presbyters of Syedra and from Palladius, that prompted the work, and is available from the publisher. It is the first complete English translation. Written in 374, shortly before the Panarion, the Ancoratus (“the firmly anchored one”) is a long letter of instruction in the faith for Christians at Syedra in Pamphylia who had asked Epiphanius for guidance: it defends the full divinity of the Son and of the Holy Spirit against the Arians and their successors, treats the Incarnation and the resurrection of the body, criticises Origen’s interpretation of Scripture, and discusses the relation of the faith to pagan religion and culture, before closing with two creeds, one of them the baptismal creed then in use and the other a longer one composed by Epiphanius himself. (Noted by Roger Pearse, 25 May 2015.)
