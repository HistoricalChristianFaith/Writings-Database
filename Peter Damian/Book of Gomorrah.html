Book of Gomorrah

No public-domain English translation of this work is available. The first complete translation, by Pierre J. Payer, is Book of Gomorrah: An Eleventh-Century Treatise against Clerical Homosexual Practices (Wilfrid Laurier University Press, 1982; ISBN 978-0-88920-123-1), with an introduction and notes and a translation of Pope Leo IX's letter in reply; it is available from the publisher. The work is also translated complete as Letter 31 in Owen J. Blum, Peter Damian: Letters 31–60 (Fathers of the Church, Mediaeval Continuation 2; Catholic University of America Press, 1990; ISBN 978-0-8132-0707-0), available from the publisher, and by Matthew Cullinan Hoffman, The Book of Gomorrah and St. Peter Damian's Struggle against Ecclesiastical Corruption (Ite ad Thomam Books and Media, 2015; ISBN 978-0-9967042-0-5), with a biographical introduction. Peter Damian, the Italian monk, reformer and later cardinal, addressed the Liber Gomorrhianus to Pope Leo IX around 1049–1051. In the first part he describes the sexual sins he found among the clergy, argues that the penances laid down in the penitential books were too lenient, and asks that offenders be removed from clerical office; in the second he appeals to the offenders themselves to repent and do penance. (Noted by Roger Pearse, 25 July 2018.)
