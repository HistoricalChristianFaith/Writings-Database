Life of Saint Dionysius the Areopagite

No public-domain English translation of this work is available. The first English translation, issued without a named translator, is The Life of Saint Dionysius the Areopagite (Scriptorium Press, 2024, second edition; ISBN 9798877337213; first edition 2023, ISBN 9798869832252); it is available from the publisher. The book gives a complete translation of the Greek encomium of Dionysius (BHG 556) transmitted under the name of Michael Syncellus (d. 846), together with the Latin Passion of Saints Dionysius, Rusticus and Eleutherius and a long introduction defending the apostolic date of the Dionysian writings. The encomium is a ninth-century Byzantine life of Dionysius, the Athenian convert of Paul in Acts 17:34, which follows him from Athens to his mission and martyrdom in Gaul, identifying him with Saint Denis of Paris. (Noted by Roger Pearse, 3 December 2024.)
