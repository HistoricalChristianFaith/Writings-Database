Life of Saint Bede the Venerable

No public-domain English translation of this work is available. The first English translation, by Anthony Pavoni and Evangelos Nikitopoulos, is The Life of Saint Bede the Venerable (Montreal: Scriptorium Press, 2023; ISBN 9798869903341), a short paperback that also contains an introduction on Bede and the history of Christianity in Britain, Symeon of Durham’s account of the translation of Bede’s relics from Jarrow to Durham, a list of Bede’s works and a bibliography; it is available from the publisher. The translation covers the complete Life (pp. 37–61). The Vita Bedae (BHL 1069) is an anonymous Latin life of Bede (d. 735) written in the north of England towards the end of the eleventh century. The Latin text was printed by John Smith in his 1722 edition of the Ecclesiastical History and reprinted by J. A. Giles, The Complete Works of Venerable Bede, vol. 1 (London, 1843), pp. cxliii–clx (Google Books); there is as yet no critical edition. (Noted by Roger Pearse, 17 December 2024, who checked a sample of the translation against the Latin and found it accurate; see also his post of 3 December 2024.)
