Life of Saint Simeon the Stylite

No public-domain English translation of this work is available. The first English translation of the Metaphrastic Life (BHG 1686) is The Life of Saint Simeon the Stylite by Simeon the Metaphrast (Scriptorium Press, 2024; ISBN 979-8-3008-9931-8), a complete translation issued without a translator’s name, with historical notes; it is available from the publisher. Symeon Metaphrastes, a high official at the Byzantine court in the later tenth century, compiled a great collection of saints’ lives for the church year, rewriting the older accounts in a more polished Greek; his collection became the standard one in the Byzantine Church. His Life of Simeon the Stylite the Elder (d. 459), the Syrian ascetic who spent decades standing on a pillar near Antioch and drew pilgrims from across the eastern Mediterranean and beyond, recasts the earlier lives of the saint into what became his classic Byzantine biography. It is a different work from the Syriac Life of Simeon, which is in this collection in Frederick Lent’s translation. (Noted by Roger Pearse, 3 December 2024.)
