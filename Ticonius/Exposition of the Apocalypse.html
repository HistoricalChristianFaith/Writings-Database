Exposition of the Apocalypse

No public-domain English translation of this work is available. The work, as reconstructed by Roger Gryson, was translated by Francis X. Gumerlock as Exposition of the Apocalypse, with an introduction and notes by David C. Robinson (The Fathers of the Church 134; Catholic University of America Press, 2017; ISBN 978-0-8132-2956-0; paperback 2026, ISBN 978-0-8132-4193-7); it is available from the publisher. It translates the whole of the reconstructed text. Ticonius (or Tyconius), a Donatist lay theologian of Roman Africa in the later fourth century, best known for his Book of Rules for interpreting Scripture, wrote a commentary on Revelation that read the book as a description of the Church in the present age rather than a timetable of the end. The commentary itself is lost apart from fragments, but later Latin commentators drew heavily on it; Gryson’s reconstruction rebuilds it from those fragments and from the writers who used it, above all Primasius of Hadrumetum, so the text translated is a modern reconstruction rather than a surviving work. (Noted by Roger Pearse, 28 June 2019; see also his post of 30 June 2019.)
